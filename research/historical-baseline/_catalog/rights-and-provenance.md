# Rights and provenance

The inventory contains original bibliographic descriptions, research plans and links. It does not include third-party scans, photographs, audio, book chapters or datasets.

| Registry field | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| `access_status` | How the source can be consulted; may include reading-room, subscription or technical restrictions |
| `full_text_status` | What was actually inspected in this research pass |
| `license_status` | A verified licence/advisory, or `not-established` when no applicable reuse grant was established |
| `license_url` | The checked rights statement, when available |
| `license_scope` | The material to which that statement applies: text, metadata, scans, dataset or particular images |
| `rights_notes` | Attribution, exclusions and unresolved item-specific questions |

Public access, historical age and institutional ownership of an object do not by themselves establish permission to republish a digital reproduction. Catalogue text, photographed objects, photographs, transcriptions, translations and editorial additions may have different rights. Follow the actual item statement and preserve uncertainty instead of assigning a blanket licence.

Before any later binary import, follow the repository [asset workflow](../../../studio/ASSET_WORKFLOW.md), including the required adjacent `.asset.json` and verified SHA-256. This source-planning phase creates no such binary imports.

The source registry records bibliographic provenance. [research-protocol.md](research-protocol.md) records AI authorship, human review status, task scope and language instructions. A source ID is not a human approval record.
