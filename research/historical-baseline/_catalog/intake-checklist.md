# Source intake requirements

This checklist applies to admitting a source for planning, not to approving historical facts.

1. Identify the exact work, edition, dataset version, accession, document or discovery collection on an authoritative institutional or scholarly site.
2. Check that its stated scope can contribute to a named 1910–1940 topic; label earlier or later context.
3. Record the creator, custodian, publication information, canonical URL and persistent identifier when available.
4. State the authority basis, appropriate evidentiary use and source-specific limits. Distinguish official records from independent scholarly analysis.
5. Record what was actually inspected, on which date, and whether the full text or item was reached.
6. Separate reading access from reuse rights; record the licence page and scope when established, otherwise preserve uncertainty.
7. Search the register for an existing edition or identifier before issuing a stable new ID. Keep aliases for merged duplicates.
8. Add verification evidence and at least one concrete planned extraction with a real topic path. Do not invent a locator.
9. Record material gaps, exclusions and unsuccessful access where these affect the plan.
10. Regenerate views and run `manage_catalog.py --check` and the repository validator.

Admission does not authorize historical claim extraction, importing third-party assets, changing canon or claiming human approval. Subsequent factual work needs exact supporting passages and a separate review record.
