## What still needs source verification

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-25 block-30 -->

Separate verification is needed for details of the Kyiv assassination attempt, accounts of Nicholas II’s response, the composition and actual capabilities of political forces in 1911, claims about Rasputin’s foreign connections, the history of Shustov’s advertising practices, specific enterprises and machine brands, regional transport routes, industrial employment, and relationships with landownership. None of these areas is confirmed merely by being mentioned in the authors’ discussion.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-25 block-31 -->

For literary and screen references, the document records the function of the influence stated by the authors. Book counts, judgments of popularity, creation histories, and biographical claims about authors are not foundations of canon and are not included as verified facts.

## The minimum record of a research finding

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-25 block-33 -->

Every new historical statement must have a clear connection to its source: title, author or institution, date, an accessible link or archival reference, the specific position supported, and limits of applicability. Where sources conflict, the disagreement remains until checked; usefulness to the plot is not used as a criterion of historical accuracy.
