# Typography and inscription requirements

[Русский](typography-brief-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1](../../../world/world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md) · Source status labels: SASHA'S FRAME; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL; OPEN GAP.

A pre-revolutionary character is required for the world’s signs, titles, emblems and documents. Typefaces have not yet been chosen; font files have not been uploaded.

## Check each candidate

- Cyrillic and Latin characters, numerals, punctuation, styles and legibility at small sizes.
- Required pre-reform characters: Ѣ ѣ І і Ѳ ѳ Ѵ ѵ Ъ ъ. The presence of a character does not establish correct spelling: check the specific text separately.
- Rights to redistribute the file, print, embed on the web, use in a game, modify and distribute derivative versions.
- Texture and behaviour of inscriptions on metal, cloth, stone and paper: perspective, curvature, application method and overall surface wear.

Historical names, old-orthography rules and dates of use are researched from sources. A modern legible UI is permissible as a separate functional system after an authorial decision. Do not convert the entire working GitHub corpus into old orthography.

Candidates are recorded in the [register](font-register.csv). For each selected typeface, use the [font specification](../../../templates/font.md).
