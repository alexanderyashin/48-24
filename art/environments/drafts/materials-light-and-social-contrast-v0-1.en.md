# Materials, light and social contrast

[Русский](materials-light-and-social-contrast-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §16](../../../world/world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-16) · Source status labels: SASHA'S FRAME; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_visual_materials"></a>

SASHA'S FRAME. Wealthy conservatives may look dignified: clean clothes, education, orderly interiors and access to comfort. Poor characters bear traces of fuel and labour. This contrast does not assign the wealthy vice in advance or the poor moral superiority. Their actions must retain independent significance. [П4]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Differences between environments can be expressed through the standard of care applied to the same material culture. In an affluent space metal is cleaned, glass intact, mechanisms adjusted and surfaces regularly repaired. In a poor one, temporary fixes, wear, repeated reuse and enforced proximity to an energy source are visible. Social meaning then emerges from treatment of objects rather than arbitrary additions of gears.

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. A colour system may distinguish light sources, time of day and environmental pollution while preserving a restrained overall palette. Cool electric light, warm gas lighting and industrial heat are currently possible staging tools, not mandatory colour codes for all factions. Exact hues, contrast and degree of painterliness are unapproved.

| Environment | Established meaning | Proposed means of expression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Affluent district | Access to cleanliness and comfort | Care of materials, space and stable light |
| Industrial district | Labour and costs displaced here | Layers of soot, crowding, repairs and nearby machinery |
| Mining and metallurgical district | Connection between production and life | Factory details repeated in housing and the urban environment |
| State residence | Governance and its rituals | Access routes, distance, sounds of communications and apparatus operation |
| Peripheral route | Dependence on distance and supply | Transport wear, season, stops, cargo and servicing personnel |
