# Stable artistic direction

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §16](../../../world/world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-16) · Source status labels: SASHA'S FRAME; JOINTLY DEVELOPED; IVAN'S PROPOSAL; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_visual_basis"></a>

SASHA'S FRAME. The named reference is Iron Sky, somewhat darker, with coal-black cities, visible air pollution and a heavy industrial environment. IVAN'S PROPOSAL is steam-powered Gothic noir in a Russian folkloric idiom. The formula describes a desired combination of mood, architecture and materiality, but does not provide a ready-made set of decorative symbols. [П3, П4]

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. The visual environment should express a way of life: coal, steam, gas lighting, slow aircraft, factory quarters, luxurious interiors and traces of hard labour. Aesthetics have a causal connection with the world’s structure. Pollution must nevertheless not destroy image legibility, and class membership cannot be reduced to one clothing colour.

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Existing styles from other projects, including LOGION, do not automatically become this universe’s visual canon. Adopting any specific technique requires a deliberate choice: it must serve the imperial alternative history, its objects and people. An independent system of costume, inscriptions, state symbols and architectural alterations remains to be developed.
