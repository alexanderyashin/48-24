# The ruler’s body and the machine’s space

[Русский](ruler-body-and-machine-space-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §16](../../../world/world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-16) · Source status labels: CONSENSUS; JOINTLY DEVELOPED; IVAN'S PROPOSAL; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_visual_body"></a>

CONSENSUS. Stolypin’s mechanisation happens gradually; at the beginning of the main story he has not yet completely lost human appearance and behaviour. The relationship between bodily transformation and political hardening is JOINTLY DEVELOPED. The precise sequence of operations, appearance of body parts and point of final immobility are not approved. [П5, П6]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Visual change may be built through growing dependence on surrounding space: first a separate device assists the person; then servicing determines his routine; later the room becomes part of his body and power. This is a possible principle, not a finished list of stages. Each stage needs to preserve personal recognizability so that the audience observes the transformation of one individual.

IVAN'S PROPOSAL. The automatic signature, voice through a mechanical device, telegraph tape and immobile throne were originally developed for the mechanical tsar, then partly incorporated into the Chancellor’s image. They cannot automatically be assigned to both figures. Particularly expressive objects require a decision about their owner; otherwise two characters acquire the same function and visual language. [П3, П4]
