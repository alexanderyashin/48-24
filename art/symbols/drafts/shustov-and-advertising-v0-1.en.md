SASHA'S FRAME. Shustov cognac should be insistently omnipresent as a recurring sign of advertising, commerce and capitalist everyday life. This is one of the correspondence’s most definite object-based motifs. Its role extends beyond a random bottle: the brand should be a recognizable part of the environment, recurring in different social situations. Specific advertising media, historical layouts and the company’s subsequent fate still require development. [П7]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Repetition may work through contrasting contexts: a prestigious product in one place, old packaging in another, an advertisement’s promise of a beautiful life and the traces of practical use in everyday surroundings. These are staging possibilities, not scenes already written. At transitions between eras, it is useful to decide separately whether the brand’s ownership, advertising language and audience change. Historical stories about Shustov’s marketing techniques require verification and are not included as established events solely on the basis of the correspondence.
