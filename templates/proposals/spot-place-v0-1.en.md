## Location or production-district card

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-23 block-4 -->

Fields: real location; name in the selected era; territorial status; composition of settlements; owner and administrators; principal products; fuel and energy sources; water requirements; population size and composition; workers' origins; engineering training; incoming and outgoing transport routes; seasonal limits; freedom of movement; armed security; relations with the center; internal opposition; everyday appearance; story functions.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-23 block-5 -->

All quantitative fields are filled only after selecting a scale and a basis for calculation. The first version may use qualitative dependencies, such as ‘critically dependent on a single rail outlet,’ provided that the existence and direction of that outlet are established. Specific throughput must not be invented merely to complete the table.
