## Character card

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-23 block-10 -->

Fields: identifier; name and variants; age in reference years; origins; social position; institutional affiliations; public role; personal objective; obligations; fear; cost of refusal; competencies; limits; knowledge; relationships; initial state; key choices; change in convictions; permitted limits of change; appearance by stage; speech; known scenes; open questions.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-23 block-11 -->

Social function and personal motivation are recorded separately. A character may express a group's interests while conflicting with its leadership. When changing sides, show which interest remains, what has changed in the circumstances, and what price the person accepts.
