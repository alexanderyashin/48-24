## Technology card

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-23 block-7 -->

Fields: name; real or fictional basis; year of introduction in this history; creators and manufacturers; energy and consumables; capabilities; limits; maintenance; frequency of failures within a range relevant to the work; availability; cost relative to other solutions; military and civilian uses; who controls its spread; effects on society; permitted visual features.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-23 block-8 -->

It is particularly important to record what the technology can and cannot do. If such a capability is available to the ruler, define the consequences of its spread or the constraints that prevent widespread use. The explanation may involve resources, reproducibility, risk, secrecy, or the organization of power, but it must be chosen explicitly.
