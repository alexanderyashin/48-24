## Canon-position card

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-23 block-2 -->

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Identifier and title | A permanent number and a short subject-specific title. |
| Statement | One verifiable idea, without combining incompatible alternatives. |
| Status | The exact label from the accepted vocabulary. |
| Scope | Era, territory, institution, characters, or technology. |
| Basis | The basis for authorial status; separately, the historical foundation and the limits of its applicability. |
| Dependencies | Which positions this entry needs, and which positions depend on it. |
| Consequences of change | Characters, scenes, places, and images requiring review. |
| Question to close | What specifically remains to be selected or established. |
