## Historical-research card

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-23 block-20 -->

Fields: question; initial hypothesis; period before or after the divergence; preferred primary sources; facts found; level of confidence; source limitations; implications for the world; possible alternatives; the decision the research makes possible. Historical fact and authorial inference must remain in separate fields.
