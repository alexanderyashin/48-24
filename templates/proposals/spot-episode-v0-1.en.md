## Episode card

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-23 block-15 -->

Fields: working title; format and duration; position in the chronology; point of view; emotional promise; the protagonist's immediate objective; obstacle; choice; turn; outcome; necessary world knowledge; new information; state changes; characters and locations; production constraints; approval status.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-23 block-16 -->

A complete episode should change at least one significant state: a participant's position, a relationship, access to a resource, knowledge, or the political situation. An atmospheric sketch may serve a separate purpose of introducing the world; it should be identified as such, without an obligation to imitate a full plot.
