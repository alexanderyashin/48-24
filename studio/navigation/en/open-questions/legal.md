## OQ-0088 — Terms review before the first commercial agreement

**Deferred until condition** · Review and agreement · Owner: Unassigned

**Question.** Do the coauthor agreement and external-contribution terms fit the actual participants, jurisdictions and intended uses of the first commercial agreement?

**Closure criterion.** Before a specific deal, its targeted review outcome is recorded and only justified agreed changes are made; today’s Draft is not presented as legally approved.

**Activation condition.** A specific commercial deal or broad rights grant for joint materials is proposed, with actual participants and intended uses identified.
