## Proposals and unresolved divergences

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-78 -->

<a id="can037"></a>

### CAN037 The mentor as an old associate

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-79 -->

Status: SASHA'S PROPOSAL. The mentor may be a senior participant in Stolypin's earlier operations and a holder of dangerous knowledge. His position, age, relationship to the royal family, and degree of personal closeness have not been agreed. Basis: [П5] [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-80 -->

<a id="can038"></a>

### CAN038 The mentor's death as a turning point

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-81 -->

Status: SASHA'S PROPOSAL. The mentor's execution or murder is proposed as the protagonist's personal breaking point with the regime. Ivan explicitly leaves this part unapproved; the mentor saving the protagonist at the cost of his own life is likewise not fixed. Basis: [П5] [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-82 -->

<a id="can039"></a>

### CAN039 A bureaucratic adversary

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-83 -->

Status: SASHA'S PROPOSAL. The head of a repressive service, acting out of professionalism and career interest, is proposed as the protagonist's direct opponent. His nationality, biography, and final place in the character system remain open. Basis: [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-84 -->

<a id="can040"></a>

### CAN040 A unified special service

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-85 -->

Status: SASHA'S PROPOSAL. Foreign operations and domestic suppression could be connected through one service whose leadership changes. This proposal simplifies character continuity, but the organization of the agencies has not been accepted by both authors. Basis: [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-86 -->

<a id="can041"></a>

### CAN041 A Union of production associations

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-87 -->

Status: SASHA'S PROPOSAL. The new state is proposed to be built around production associations in which workers participate in ownership. The connection between the districts and the future corporations is sketched out; citizens' rights and the structure of supreme power are not defined. Basis: [П1] [П7].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-88 -->

<a id="can042"></a>

### CAN042 Global victory and subsequent breakup

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-89 -->

Status: SASHA'S PROPOSAL. The new Union's global victory and its breakup after the disappearance of a common opponent belong to the early overarching arc. This geopolitical sequence has not become an agreed outcome for both coauthors. Basis: [П1] [П4].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-90 -->

<a id="can043"></a>

### CAN043 A local infection storyline

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-91 -->

Status: SASHA'S PROPOSAL. A local infection story in a remote village is allowed within a world that otherwise continues to exist. A fungal origin, the specific location, the infected freezing to death, and the connection to the larger story have not been approved. Basis: [П4].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-92 -->

<a id="can044"></a>

### CAN044 A second protagonist as ruler of the Wastelands

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-93 -->

Status: IVAN'S PROPOSAL. An internal-security officer may receive an independent arc as ruler of the Wastelands and support the revolution with supplies. This version has not been reconciled with the proposal to make him the protagonist's principal pursuer. Basis: [П5] [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-94 -->

<a id="can045"></a>

### CAN045 The worker, noblewoman, and doctor

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-95 -->

Status: IVAN'S PROPOSAL. The storylines of the worker, noblewoman, and doctor with his sick wife remain story sketches. Their centrality, their connection to the intelligence officers, and whether literal infection is retained have not yet been defined. Basis: [П1] [П2].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-96 -->

<a id="can046"></a>

### CAN046 Mechanical Alexei

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-97 -->

Status: DIVERGENCE. Alexei's candidacy previously received direct agreement. Later, the central mechanization concept was transferred to Stolypin without a separate decision on Alexei's fate. The existence of two mechanized rulers cannot be asserted. Basis: [П3] [П4] [П5].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-98 -->

<a id="can047"></a>

### CAN047 Transferring consciousness into a mechanism

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-99 -->

Status: IVAN'S PROPOSAL. The transfer of Stolypin's consciousness into a computing installation has been described. This is one technical interpretation of mechanization; its compatibility with a surviving organism, human will, and the capabilities of the period has not yet been defined. Basis: [П4].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-100 -->

<a id="can048"></a>

### CAN048 Flashbacks through the Chancellor's memory

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-101 -->

Status: IVAN'S PROPOSAL. The past may be revealed through memories of the mechanized Stolypin triggered by present events. Exclusive use of this device for the entire backstory, and the reliability of those memories, have not been agreed. Basis: [П4].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-102 -->

<a id="can049"></a>

### CAN049 Ecclesiastical positions toward the regime

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-103 -->

Status: IVAN'S PROPOSAL. Supporters of a saving order and defenders of traditional monarchical legitimacy have been proposed within the church. Their institutions, compromises, and response to mechanization require development. Basis: [П4] [П6].
