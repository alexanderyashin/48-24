# Source consensus: CAN001–CAN014

[Русский](consensus-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** A normative draft for collaborative development. Authorial status applies to the exact source statement; migration and translation do not create new coauthor agreement. Translation awaits human review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §3](../../../world/world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-3) · [Current canon policy](../../CANON_POLICY.md).

Source statuses: CONSENSUS.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-0 -->

This register records the degree of authorial acceptance of positions, not their historical or technical accuracy. CONSENSUS applies only to the accepted wording; its details may remain open. The evidence is identified by the numbers of the seven supplied batches. Editorial analysis does not promote a proposal to shared canon.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-1 -->

An earlier-version entry preserves an element's provenance and prevents its accidental reintroduction. CANCELLED is used only where there was an explicit rejection. An undiscussed extension of an idea, a positive response to a general direction, or a request to assemble a document does not imply acceptance of every detail.

## Accepted positions

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-3 -->

<a id="can001"></a>

### CAN001 The divergence in 1911

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-4 -->

Status: CONSENSUS. The assassination attempt in Kyiv does not kill Stolypin. His survival is accepted as the initial divergence of the alternate history. The composition of the conspiracy, the circumstances of his survival, and the political consequences need separate elaboration. Basis: [П2] [П3] [П4].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-5 -->

<a id="can002"></a>

### CAN002 The beginning in 1941

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-6 -->

Status: CONSENSUS. The main narrative begins in 1941. This is the audience's entry point into the changed world, not an automatically established starting date for every war, act of repression, or technological transformation. Basis: [П4] [П5].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-7 -->

<a id="can003"></a>

### CAN003 Backstory through flashbacks

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-8 -->

Status: CONSENSUS. Events before 1941 belong to the backstory and are revealed mainly through flashbacks. Their limited screen time does not remove the need for an internal chronology and causal connections. Basis: [П4] [П5] [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-9 -->

<a id="can004"></a>

### CAN004 Coal and steam as the basis of the environment

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-10 -->

Status: CONSENSUS. Coal-based energy and steam machinery define the empire's recognizable industrial environment. Specific capacities, machine types, and the distribution of technologies have not yet been established. Basis: [П3] [П4].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-11 -->

<a id="can005"></a>

### CAN005 The existence of electricity

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-12 -->

Status: CONSENSUS. Electricity exists and is unevenly distributed. The visible difference in access is agreed; the precise extent of electrification, industrial use, and state policy remain open. Basis: [П4] [П7].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-13 -->

<a id="can006"></a>

### CAN006 Social contrast in the visuals

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-14 -->

Status: CONSENSUS. The cleanliness, material security, and education of affluent groups contrast with the soot, dangerous living conditions, and exhausting labor of the poor. This contrast must be legible through the environment, lighting, clothing, and conditions of life. Basis: [П4].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-15 -->

<a id="can007"></a>

### CAN007 Stolypin's gradual mechanization

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-16 -->

Status: CONSENSUS. Stolypin is mechanized in stages. Individual changes to his body and functions accompany his political evolution; complete mechanization must not absorb every stage of this arc in advance. Basis: [П5] [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-17 -->

<a id="can008"></a>

### CAN008 Human qualities at the beginning of the arc

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-18 -->

Status: CONSENSUS. At the beginning of the main narrative, Stolypin still retains human qualities and personal ties. Implants are possible; the exact degree of mechanization in the opening scene has not been defined. Basis: [П5] [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-19 -->

<a id="can009"></a>

### CAN009 Increasing severity alongside mechanization

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-20 -->

Status: CONSENSUS. As the crisis develops, Stolypin becomes less tolerant of disorder and his government more repressive. Complete mechanization is linked to a late stage of this process. Specific crimes and decisions have not yet been laid out. Basis: [П5] [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-21 -->

<a id="can010"></a>

### CAN010 Rasputin in the backstory

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-22 -->

Status: CONSENSUS. Rasputin participates in the backstory of the regime's establishment. His inclusion is accepted, but his role as an independent opponent or a controlled instrument of Stolypin remains a divergence. Basis: [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-23 -->

<a id="can011"></a>

### CAN011 A large world and individual stories

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-24 -->

Status: CONSENSUS. The world is intended to support many independent stories within a shared historical framework. An individual installment may follow local characters without including the central protagonist. Basis: [П4].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-25 -->

<a id="can012"></a>

### CAN012 Airships as part of the action

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-26 -->

Status: CONSENSUS. Airships must participate in the action. An adventure or detective episode aboard one has support as a direction; the specific murder, passengers, and resolution have not been approved. Basis: [П3] [П5].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-27 -->

<a id="can013"></a>

### CAN013 A real geographical foundation

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-28 -->

Status: CONSENSUS. Real Russia provides the geographical basis. The final part of the discussion supports working with actual places and developing their alternate history. Political borders and place names at different dates still need to be checked. Basis: [П7].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-29 -->

<a id="can014"></a>

### CAN014 The need for an economic map

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-30 -->

Status: CONSENSUS. The world needs industrial geography, resources, transport, and a supply system for the capital. Research is expected to follow interconnected territories and industries, rather than only visually impressive objects. Basis: [П6] [П7].
