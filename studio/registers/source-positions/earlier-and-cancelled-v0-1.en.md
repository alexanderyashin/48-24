## Earlier and explicitly cancelled versions

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-105 -->

<a id="can050"></a>

### CAN050 A global First World War epidemic

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-106 -->

Status: EARLIER VERSION. Worldwide infection and quarantine were proposed as causes of an unfinished war and technological reconfiguration. This foundation was not accepted into the shared line; it is not a hidden active cause of the later world. Basis: [П1] [П2] [П3].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-107 -->

<a id="can051"></a>

### CAN051 Aristocratic magic versus technology

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-108 -->

Status: EARLIER VERSION. Inherited magical abilities and the suppression of magic by technological development were discussed. The later construction does not assign this law or a set of abilities to the social classes. Basis: [П1] [П2].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-109 -->

<a id="can052"></a>

### CAN052 Nicholas as a clandestine mechanical tsar

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-110 -->

Status: EARLIER VERSION. Nicholas was proposed as a rival to Stolypin preserved within an installation. Alexei replaced him as the candidate during discussion; details of the original version must not be transferred to Alexei automatically. Basis: [П3].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-111 -->

<a id="can053"></a>

### CAN053 Complete mechanization by 1941

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-112 -->

Status: EARLIER VERSION. In one text, Stolypin has already been fully transformed into an installation by the start of the action. Subsequent direct agreement establishes gradual mechanization and the retention of human qualities at the beginning. Basis: [П4] [П5].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-113 -->

<a id="can054"></a>

### CAN054 A plantation United States and a fragmented Germany

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-114 -->

Status: EARLIER VERSION. The early geopolitical sketches have not been reconciled with the later 1911 divergence and subsequent European configurations. These states of the powers are not considered a finished world map. Basis: [П1] [П2].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-115 -->

<a id="can055"></a>

### CAN055 Early dates for defeat and revolution

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-116 -->

Status: EARLIER VERSION. The reference points of defeat in 1945 and overthrow in 1948 were preliminary. The later need for a prolonged crisis and underground struggle requires a new shared timeline. Basis: [П4] [П5] [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-117 -->

<a id="can056"></a>

### CAN056 The paternal version of the characters' relationship

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-118 -->

Status: CANCELLED. The paternal relationship under discussion was withdrawn by Ivan's explicit statement. The full original version is absent from the supplied material; no additional facts about the protagonist's family can be inferred from its cancellation. Basis: [П5].
