## Jointly developed positions

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-59 -->

<a id="can028"></a>

### CAN028 Mining and metallurgical districts

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-60 -->

Status: JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Privately owned industrial districts and factory towns are considered the spatial and social basis of the Urals. Their autonomy, the limits of ownership, and their subordination to the state have not yet been defined. Basis: [П7].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-61 -->

<a id="can029"></a>

### CAN029 Resources and transport as sources of conflict

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-62 -->

Status: JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Ore, coal, and delivery routes connect the capital, factories, and remote territories. Disrupted supply must have consequences; actual routes and volumes have not yet been calculated. Basis: [П6] [П7].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-63 -->

<a id="can030"></a>

### CAN030 Workers and the engineering stratum

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-64 -->

Status: JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Industry requires an explanation for its mass labor force and the origins of qualified specialists. Penal labor is proposed as one source of workers, but a complete system of employment and education is missing. Basis: [П7].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-65 -->

<a id="can031"></a>

### CAN031 Smuggling and piracy

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-66 -->

Status: JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Smuggling networks and attacks on cargo are considered part of both the economy and the adventures. Drugs, raw-material exports, and luxury imports have different foundations and are not reduced to one universal criminal network. Basis: [П7].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-67 -->

<a id="can032"></a>

### CAN032 A foreign-intelligence protagonist

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-68 -->

Status: JOINTLY DEVELOPED. One of the central storylines concerns a foreign-intelligence officer who is initially loyal to Stolypin. His exceptional qualities, career, and relationship with a possible second protagonist have not yet been fixed. Basis: [П5] [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-69 -->

<a id="can033"></a>

### CAN033 A prolonged crisis of the regime

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-70 -->

Status: JOINTLY DEVELOPED. The regime's breakdown should take years and have several stages. Ten to fifteen years and a more detailed two-period model were discussed; no single approved dating scheme exists yet. Basis: [П5] [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-71 -->

<a id="can034"></a>

### CAN034 Reformer and guardian of order

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-72 -->

Status: JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Stolypin combines the intention to transform the country with a need to preserve the order he has created. His early successes and later repression must form a causal arc, rather than two disconnected personalities. Basis: [П3] [П4] [П5].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-73 -->

<a id="can035"></a>

### CAN035 Memory and the loss of humanity

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-74 -->

Status: JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Memories of earlier relationships and service contribute to the drama of mechanization. It remains to be decided whether emotions are genuine experiences, residual responses, or Stolypin's own later interpretation. Basis: [П4] [П5].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-75 -->

<a id="can036"></a>

### CAN036 The outside world through intelligence work

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-76 -->

Status: JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Intelligence stories should reveal Russia's relationships with other powers. The importance of the international dimension is recognized, but final alliances, borders, and the sequence of international conflicts have not been established. Basis: [П3] [П5] [П6].
