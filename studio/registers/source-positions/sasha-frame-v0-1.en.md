## Sasha's frames

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-32 -->

<a id="can015"></a>

### CAN015 A continuation of real history

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-33 -->

Status: SASHA'S FRAME. The real historical baseline applies up to the accepted point of divergence. Subsequent changes must have causes within the world; recognizable Russia is not replaced by an arbitrary map with decorative Russian names. Basis: [П1] [П2] [П7].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-34 -->

<a id="can016"></a>

### CAN016 A function for every introduced detail

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-35 -->

Status: SASHA'S FRAME. Significant technologies, territories, institutions, and characters are introduced for an artistic or causal function. How strictly this principle applies to background details of everyday life has not been separately defined. Basis: [П1] [П2].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-36 -->

<a id="can017"></a>

### CAN017 One overarching protagonist

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-37 -->

Status: SASHA'S FRAME. One central human arc connects the entire historical cycle. Independent protagonists in individual stories are allowed. Equal standing for two overarching protagonists has not been finally agreed with Ivan. Basis: [П1] [П4] [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-38 -->

<a id="can018"></a>

### CAN018 From service to a system of his own

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-39 -->

Status: SASHA'S FRAME. The protagonist moves from support for the Chancellor, through revolution, to building his own state and eventually becoming historically obsolete. The specific beliefs and actions at each stage have not yet been defined. Basis: [П4] [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-40 -->

<a id="can019"></a>

### CAN019 Two historical cycles

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-41 -->

Status: SASHA'S FRAME. Stolypin's cycle and the protagonist's cycle are intended to show the emergence, development, and exhaustion of different systems. This is an authorial thematic construction, not an established law of real history. Basis: [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-42 -->

<a id="can020"></a>

### CAN020 Ending with the protagonist's death

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-43 -->

Status: SASHA'S FRAME. The central protagonist's death and the disintegration of the order he created are proposed as the endpoint of the overall work. The form of the breakup, the state's territory, and the connection between the personal death and the political process have not been agreed. Basis: [П4] [П6].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-44 -->

<a id="can021"></a>

### CAN021 The protagonist as a catalyst for revolution

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-45 -->

Status: SASHA'S FRAME. The reasons for a mass turn toward opposition must exist independently of the protagonist's personal appeal. He organizes forces that have already emerged; the regime's actions create the conditions for a revolutionary coalition. Basis: [П5] [П6] [П7].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-46 -->

<a id="can022"></a>

### CAN022 The social function of characters

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-47 -->

Status: SASHA'S FRAME. Principal characters are connected to social interests and worldviews. Their development must retain a meaningful relationship with the force they represent. The limits of individual autonomy require agreement. Basis: [П1].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-48 -->

<a id="can023"></a>

### CAN023 The narrator as a connecting presence

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-49 -->

Status: SASHA'S FRAME. The narrator is intended to connect the parts of the work and express the authors' perspective. The bearer of the voice, its degree of unreliability, and the narrator's presence in the video version have not been chosen. Basis: [П1].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-50 -->

<a id="can024"></a>

### CAN024 The cost of postponed change

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-51 -->

Status: SASHA'S FRAME. The world explores the cost of preserving the old order, lost opportunities, and accumulating contradictions. The claim that a specific revolution is inevitable serves the concept and needs to be demonstrated through the events of the work itself. Basis: [П1] [П4] [П7].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-52 -->

<a id="can025"></a>

### CAN025 Historical enterprises as material

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-53 -->

Status: SASHA'S FRAME. The preferred approach is to research real enterprises and brands, then develop them along an alternate trajectory. This principle does not justify automatically importing any later Soviet industrial site. Basis: [П7].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-54 -->

<a id="can026"></a>

### CAN026 Shustov as a recurring motif

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-55 -->

Status: SASHA'S FRAME. Shustov cognac and its advertising are to recur as recognizable motifs of consumption, enterprise, and intrusive promotion. Historical advertising episodes must be distinguished from authorial stylization. Basis: [П7].

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-56 -->

<a id="can027"></a>

### CAN027 An economic explanation for technology

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-3 block-57 -->

Status: SASHA'S FRAME. Markets, infrastructure, and return on investment are proposed as the explanation for steam's predominance. The ruler's personal hatred of electricity is not an accepted cause. The model's economic plausibility still requires development. Basis: [П2] [П4].
