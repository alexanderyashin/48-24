## DIV015 Social causes and personal madness

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-101 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha requires economic contradictions, mass dissatisfaction, and repression against the regime's own supports; the protagonist organizes a movement that has already emerged.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-102 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan develops Stolypin's fear, loss of humanity, and pursuit of absolute order. The detailed connection between these experiences and the economy has not yet been described.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-103 -->

Consequences. If the condition of one ruler becomes the sole cause, the intended systemic scale is lost. If only the economy remains, the actors' personal responsibility disappears.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-104 -->

Question for consolidation. How do personal decisions amplify existing social contradictions?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-105 -->

Closure criterion. For the key groups, the initial interest, deterioration of their position, actions of the authorities, and the specific reason for moving from loyalty to resistance are established.
