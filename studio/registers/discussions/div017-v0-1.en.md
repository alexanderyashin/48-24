## DIV017 The state after the revolution

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-115 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha proposes production associations, worker ownership, and a second cycle in which the new order becomes an obstacle to development. Global victory appears as a possible overarching arc.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-116 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan works primarily on the pre-revolutionary world and personal storylines. The correspondence contains no scheme of the future Union, global expansion, and breakup approved by him.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-117 -->

Consequences. The future model influences what the revolutionaries unite to achieve. It cannot be treated as the characters' shared program or an agreed ending solely on the basis of an early proposal.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-118 -->

Question for consolidation. What new structure actually emerges, and which promises does it fulfill?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-119 -->

Closure criterion. The unit of association, ownership, representation, the position of non-working citizens, shared services, and the causes of the later crisis are defined.
