## DIV009 Rasputin's role

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-59 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha proposes an opponent of the emerging regime: a corrupt archaic order that Stolypin exposes and destroys, using an accusation of a British conspiracy.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-60 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan wants Rasputin to become an instrument, controlled through blackmail, for discrediting the royal family and weakening the monarch's sacred authority.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-61 -->

Consequences. Rasputin's autonomy, Stolypin's moral responsibility, and the meaning of the church's response change. An accusation of foreign connections is not necessarily true within the world.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-62 -->

Question for consolidation. What are Rasputin's real objectives, and to whom is he subordinate in decisive episodes?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-63 -->

Closure criterion. His own goal, sources of influence, the substance of the blackmail or overt conflict, the reality of foreign connections, and the reason for his eventual fate are defined.
