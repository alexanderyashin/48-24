## DIV007 Stages of final mechanization

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-45 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha proposes connecting the final transformation to the protagonist's failed assassination attempt and the emergence of a new head of the repressive service.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-46 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan accepts gradual change and considers another assassination attempt a possible trigger against the backdrop of the approaching revolution. The specific episode and its date have not been approved.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-47 -->

Consequences. The agreed direction still allows different causal sequences. These determine responsibility for escalation and when the protagonist understands the consequences of his action.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-48 -->

Question for consolidation. Which event triggers the last irreversible stage of transformation?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-49 -->

Closure criterion. A sequence connects the external event, Stolypin's decision, the technical change, and the political consequence; what the participants and audience know is marked.
