## DIV012 The internal-security officer

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-80 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha proposes a bureaucratic pursuer and active representative of the mechanical regime who can obstruct the protagonist personally.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-81 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan sees an independent protagonist, a future ruler of the Wastelands, who helps the revolution and later clashes with the new ruler. Replacing him with an adversary has not been accepted.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-82 -->

Consequences. One figure receives functions that are currently incompatible: official enemy, independent ally, and later opponent. Combining them requires a separate causal arc.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-83 -->

Question for consolidation. Is this one changing character, or different roles belonging to different people?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-84 -->

Closure criterion. The figure's service, interests, and relationship to the protagonist at each stage are defined, or the functions are explicitly distributed among several characters.
