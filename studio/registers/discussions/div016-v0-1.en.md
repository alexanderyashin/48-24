## DIV016 Duration of the crisis and generations

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-108 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha progressively extends the time allowed for the regime's decline, independent underground activity, and the construction of a new state. A later estimate includes roughly ten years followed by another five to seven.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-109 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan confirms a prolonged interval and an age of around twenty-five at the start, retaining 1941 as the beginning. A complete calendar scheme has not been accepted.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-110 -->

Consequences. The mentor's age, Stolypin's physical condition, the associates' maturation, and the protagonist's ability to participate in later decades all change. Early dates must not be mixed with later ones.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-111 -->

Question for consolidation. What minimum and maximum intervals does each stage need?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-112 -->

Closure criterion. One chronological table contains dates or intervals, the ages of key figures, and causal transitions without mutually incompatible reference points.
