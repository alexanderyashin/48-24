## DIV014 The number and organization of intelligence and security services

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-94 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha proposes one service whose change in leadership transforms external action into the state's repression of itself, preserving continuity in the familiar cast.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-95 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan distinguishes the foreign and domestic officers as carriers of different storylines. The shared agency structure and the need to unify it have not been approved.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-96 -->

Consequences. Jurisdiction determines access to information, the legality of pursuit, and the plausibility of character encounters. Modern colloquial abbreviations cannot substitute for the agencies' organization.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-97 -->

Question for consolidation. Which functions are separated, and which are subordinate to a single leadership?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-98 -->

Closure criterion. A compact scheme of services, powers, information channels, and reporting lines allows the principal missions to be checked without changing the rules.
