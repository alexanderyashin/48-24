## DIV021 Historical enterprises and alternate development

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-143 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha requires real firms and production locations, while allowing familiar later industrial sites to survive. This is not yet a causal reconstruction of how they came into existence.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-144 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan draws on mining and metallurgical districts, Ural industrial sites he has visited, and an early electrometallurgical example. A specific list of objects has not been agreed.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-145 -->

Consequences. An enterprise may emerge after the divergence because of decisions that never occurred in this world. Geographical suitability does not prove that its name, owner, and specialization remain unchanged.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-146 -->

Question for consolidation. What is inherited from history, and what must arise anew in a different sequence?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-147 -->

Closure criterion. Each key object has its real baseline data, alternate mechanism of growth, and assumptions about transport, capital, personnel, and markets recorded.
