## DIV008 Formal monarchy and the Chancellor's power

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-52 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha proposes different arrangements, from a subordinated sovereign to a de facto power behind the throne under a mechanical monarch. Stolypin's actual power is central.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-53 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan describes emergency powers, parliamentary formalization, the family's isolation, and the subsequent rule of the Chancellor-Guardian. The exact constitutional form has not been agreed.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-54 -->

Consequences. It is unclear in whose name the army and courts act, to whom people swear allegiance, and why different groups recognize an order as lawful. A change of label without institutional consequences is insufficient.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-55 -->

Question for consolidation. Who holds supreme authority, and how does succession work?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-56 -->

Closure criterion. A scheme defines the powers of the monarch, Chancellor, representative institutions, and security bodies, together with rules for signing orders and transferring power.
