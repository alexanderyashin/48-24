## DIV011 One overarching protagonist or several

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-73 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha establishes one bearer of the complete historical arc, while allowing individual episodes without his participation.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-74 -->

Ivan's position. At different stages, Ivan proposes three personal storylines, then two central intelligence officers, and explicitly regards both as protagonists. The narrative hierarchy has not been chosen.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-75 -->

Consequences. The number of overarching arcs affects scale, points of view, the ending, and the allocation of events. Simply combining all sketches does not resolve their competition for the central function.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-76 -->

Question for consolidation. Who carries the overall arc, and which characters have independent endings of equal standing?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-77 -->

Closure criterion. A scheme of central and local arcs shows their beginnings, intersections, and conclusions without requiring detailed treatment of every episode.
