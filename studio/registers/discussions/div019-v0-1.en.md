## DIV019 Reasons for limited electrification

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-129 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha explains the spread of technology through return on investment, the size of economic units, and infrastructure; the wealthy use electricity without a principled prohibition from Stolypin.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-130 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan accepts the social picture, but in literary versions calls electricity a risky novelty and proposes communities of experimenters. Later he supplies an example of industrial electricity use.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-131 -->

Consequences. Economic expense, technical unreliability, and a state ban produce different industries and conflicting interests. Combining them without clear boundaries creates an inconsistent world.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-132 -->

Question for consolidation. What limits electricity in each sector and territory?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-133 -->

Closure criterion. A map of electricity use in homes, industry, transport, and communications specifies the separate reasons for limits and the available alternatives.
