## DIV001 Scale of the work and format of the installment

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-3 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha defines an epic with a substantial internal canon, revealed through individual texts and short videos. For him, the scale of the world precedes the choice of medium.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-4 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan aims at an accessible mass-audience series, distinctive characters, and affordable visualization. A large world is acceptable, but must support the production of specific stories.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-5 -->

Consequences. Without distinguishing internal completeness from the first installment, work may oscillate indefinitely between an encyclopedia and a pilot scene. Neither the proposed trilogy nor the episode length has been approved.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-6 -->

Question for consolidation. Which completed product will be the first test of the world, and for whom is it being made?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-7 -->

Closure criterion. The format of the first installment, the canon it necessarily requires, and the rules for expanding the shared universe without rewriting its foundations are recorded.
