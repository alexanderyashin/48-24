## DIV018 Geopolitical configuration

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-122 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha proposes several versions of Germany, Britain, and the coming war, from early fragmentation to large European blocs. Not all of them are compatible with the 1911 divergence.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-123 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan develops tension between Russia and Germany, British operations, and French revanchism. A detailed world map and sequence of alliances have not been recorded.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-124 -->

Consequences. Foreign intelligence, maritime trade, and the causes of war change with the map. The early plantation United States and Germany of principalities cannot be accepted automatically.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-125 -->

Question for consolidation. Which states exist in 1941, and what brought them to that condition?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-126 -->

Closure criterion. There is a list of key powers with their territory, regime, interests, relationships, and a short chain of changes since the point of divergence.
