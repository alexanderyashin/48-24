## DIV022 Archetype and character autonomy

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-150 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha requires principal figures to be functionally linked to social forces, and the protagonist's victory to express the transition between eras. His central protagonist changes sides more freely than the others.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-151 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan seeks independent personal stories and retains interest in an ambiguous second protagonist. A rigid allocation of social and psychological roles has not been fixed.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-152 -->

Consequences. Overly literal representation turns people into illustrations of a thesis; arbitrary changes of belief destroy the stated connection to society. A shared degree of freedom is needed.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-153 -->

Question for consolidation. Which changes in personality are allowed without losing the social function?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-154 -->

Closure criterion. For the central cast, interests, personal needs, limits of compromise, and reasons for changing positions are defined; social roles do not substitute for motivation.
