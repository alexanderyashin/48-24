## DIV002 Age of the audience and content

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-10 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha describes an action story with adult grotesquerie, sexual themes, cruelty, and black humor, while using the label young adult.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-11 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan accepts the initial tone and separately emphasizes the project's appeal to school pupils and teenagers as a mass audience. Shared content limits have not been defined.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-12 -->

Consequences. Positioning, methods of depiction, and distribution possibilities remain uncertain. The age of an adult protagonist does not by itself determine the audience's age.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-13 -->

Question for consolidation. Who is the primary audience, and which methods of depiction fit that choice?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-14 -->

Closure criterion. There is a short editorial specification of the audience, tone, and limits of depiction that can be used to assess every script and visual asset.
