Source statuses: DIVERGENCE.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-0 -->

This register includes both direct disagreements and alternatives that have not been reconciled. The existence of a card does not mean the coauthors must argue over the question: sometimes it is enough to select one interpretation or clarify the limits of compatibility. Until that decision, the cards have DIVERGENCE status. Closure criteria are editorial proposals for collaborative work.
