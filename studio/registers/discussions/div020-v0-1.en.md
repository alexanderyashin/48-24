## DIV020 What the Wastelands mean

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-136 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha insists on real geography and economic districts, linking remote territories to resources, exile, and revolutionary potential.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-137 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan proposes a frontier space with free communities, piracy, camps, and autonomous enclaves. The term sometimes denotes the whole region and sometimes the space between centers.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-138 -->

Consequences. Population density, transport, and authority cannot simultaneously follow the image of an uninhabited desert and a developed industrial network without spatial differentiation.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-139 -->

Question for consolidation. Which territories does the name encompass, and who actually controls each of them?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-140 -->

Closure criterion. A typology of districts, settlements, camps, and weakly controlled spaces specifies their population, functions, connections, and the limits of state power.
