## DIV006 Consciousness, will, and the machine

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-38 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha connects final mechanization to Stolypin becoming a symbol of the system and losing human agency; the direct opponent needs an independent character.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-39 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan retains the mechanized ruler's internal conflict, memory, and tragedy. His technical descriptions allow a human consciousness to function inside the installation.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-40 -->

Consequences. Responsibility for decisions, the possibility of change, the reliability of memories, and the nature of the final confrontation differ. Bodily failure does not automatically mean the disappearance of personality.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-41 -->

Question for consolidation. What exactly survives in the mechanized Stolypin, and what is lost?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-42 -->

Closure criterion. Memory, desires, the capacity for doubt, the limits of independent decision-making, and the influence of the people servicing him are defined for every stage of mechanization.
