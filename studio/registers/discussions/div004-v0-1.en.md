## DIV004 Depth of backstory development

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-24 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha considers economic and political history from 1911 onward necessary to explain the state of the world, even when it is shown only briefly.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-25 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan recognizes the need for lore, but insists on prioritizing events after 1941 and limiting the earlier decades' screen presence.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-26 -->

Consequences. A boundary is needed between information required for causality and material that can be developed later. Otherwise, already written scenes keep changing or unused detail accumulates.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-27 -->

Question for consolidation. Which pre-1941 facts are mandatory for the first version of the world?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-28 -->

Closure criterion. The backstory contains a sufficient chain of changes in power, industries, and foreign relations; optional episodes that do not affect the initial state are listed separately.
