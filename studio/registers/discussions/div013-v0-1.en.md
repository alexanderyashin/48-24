## DIV013 The mentor and the personal breaking point

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-87 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha proposes an influential associate of Stolypin whose destruction makes the protagonist's continued service impossible and connects the regime's past to its present.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-88 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan allows the idea but explicitly leaves the mentor element unapproved. His position, involvement in earlier operations, and manner of death have not been agreed.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-89 -->

Consequences. Without a decision, mandatory revolutionary dates, the composition of the conspiracy, and all colleagues' migration to the opposition cannot be built on the execution.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-90 -->

Question for consolidation. Which function of the mentor is necessary, and which event brings about the protagonist's turning point?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-91 -->

Closure criterion. There is a minimal biography, relationships with two generations of characters, and a checked causal connection between the mentor's fate and the protagonist's choice.
