## DIV010 The church and the legitimacy of the new order

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-66 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha uses the conflict between archaic and new orders and proposes the political removal of the monarchy through a struggle against the court's entourage.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-67 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan requires the significance of God's anointed sovereign to be preserved, and describes ecclesiastical sides supporting the Chancellor or defending traditional legitimacy. There is no unified institutional model.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-68 -->

Consequences. The church may become mere scenery if its assent and resistance have no consequences. At the same time, all clergy cannot be treated as a single will.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-69 -->

Question for consolidation. Which religious institutions recognize the new regime, and on what grounds?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-70 -->

Closure criterion. Influential sides, arguments for recognition and refusal, the political price of each choice, and attitudes to the ruler's mechanization are identified.
