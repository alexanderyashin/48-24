## DIV003 Limits of the speculative premise

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-17 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha requires a functional premise that explains the change in history, and rejects additional abilities introduced merely to make a recognizable set of factions. Local horror is later allowed.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-18 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan begins with practical magic and infection, then develops the mechanization of the ruler. No final list of permitted exceptions to real nature has been compiled.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-19 -->

Consequences. It is impossible to tell whether preserving consciousness is the sole exception, a reproducible industry, or an expression of a broader law of the world. The answer changes medicine, warfare, and social organization.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-20 -->

Question for consolidation. Which unusual phenomena objectively exist, and how widely can they be reproduced?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-21 -->

Closure criterion. A limited list of speculative premises is prepared, specifying their scope, cost, limits, and a list of unconfirmed rumors.
