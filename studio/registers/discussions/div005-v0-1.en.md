## DIV005 Alexei and Stolypin as mechanized rulers

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-31 -->

Sasha's position. Sasha agreed to Alexei's mechanization, then developed Stolypin's gradual transformation in detail. No separate decision to exclude Alexei followed.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-32 -->

Ivan's position. Ivan first accepted Alexei as the candidate, then transferred the central image of the mechanized sovereign to Stolypin in the next major version. The two figures' coexistence has not been described.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-33 -->

Consequences. Two such rulers change the technology's exceptional status, the legitimacy of power, and the distribution of dramatic functions. Both versions must not be combined by default.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-34 -->

Question for consolidation. Who is mechanized, and what fate is established for the remaining members of the dynasty?

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-19 block-35 -->

Closure criterion. One scheme records the status of Nicholas, Alexei, and Stolypin in the key years, including the reasons for retaining or excluding each conception.
