IVAN'S PROPOSAL. A provincial physician tries to save his wife from an illness that turns a person into a living corpse. Given hope of help in the capital, he transports her in a coffin, conceals her condition, and sustains her life. The worsening illness creates danger on the road. This strand was developed within the early infection model; its transfer into the later world has not been confirmed. [П1]

The physician's proposed arc moves from treatment to loss of sanity and destructive intent: releasing his ill wife in the capital and dying alongside others. Its thematic conflict includes love, professional duty, denial of loss, and responsibility towards other people. His wife is so far described mainly through her condition and her husband's actions. [П1]

DIVERGENCE: the infectious nature of the illness, preservation of the wife's consciousness, possibility of treatment, and relationship to the local Siberian story. OPEN GAP: the wife's own perspective and will, both characters' ages, and their exact chronological placement. A comic journey with a tragic outcome is proposed but not approved as the tone of the entire arc.
