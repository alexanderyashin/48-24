JOINTLY DEVELOPED. During discussion of the candidate for the mechanical tsar, Alexei receives the explicit response ‘let's do it’. His illness supplies a narrative basis for mechanisation; aristocrats and external powers may see him as a source of dynastic legitimacy. Stolypin's later transformation into a machine does not explain whether this character continues to exist alongside the Chancellor. That is a separate open divergence. [П3; П4]

IVAN'S PROPOSAL. Alexei accepts the operation out of fear, hope of relief from suffering, or a conscious wish to serve his country. These alternatives define his responsibility differently. SASHA'S PROPOSAL: mechanisation makes him the last representative of the line of succession; its reproductive consequences are part of the speculative construction, not an outcome established by a historical reference. [П3]

OPEN GAP: age at the operation, preservation of body and personhood, public or secret status, source of orders, his own political desires, and relationship with Nicholas. A puppet machine, a ruling cyborg, and a passive relic represent different character functions.
