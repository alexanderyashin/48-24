# CHAR013 The engineer and the technical community

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Engineers are necessary to operate manufacturing districts, create machines, and maintain the mechanised ruler. Proposed plots have them murdered, abducted, or transported as valuable cargo; free communities beyond the Urals may undertake research. There is not yet an individual character with an approved biography. [П3; П7]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Development should establish what binds a specialist to a workplace: professional opportunity, property, privilege, duty, dependence, or coercion. This determines whether they become a pillar of the regime, a defector, an intermediary, or a participant in a new system. Possession of knowledge does not itself imply progressive politics. [Р1]

OPEN GAP: specialist training, freedom of movement, responsibility for dangerous technologies, participation in mechanisation, and attitudes towards workers. Technical qualification must be distinguished from political programme. This niche is particularly important for explaining the development of a world that maintains complex production despite limited infrastructure.
