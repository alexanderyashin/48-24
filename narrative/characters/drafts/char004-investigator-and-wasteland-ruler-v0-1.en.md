DIVERGENCE. This card preserves two directions that must not be silently combined. IVAN'S PROPOSAL: a domestic security officer becomes ruler of the Wastelands, supplies the revolution, retains his independence for a long time, and later clashes with the intelligence officer once the latter has become a new Stolypin. SASHA'S PROPOSAL: a security officer becomes the protagonist's active personal opponent during the revolution. [П5; П6]

In the second version, his interests are career advancement and the effective exercise of authority. He is bureaucratically ruthless, capable of pursuing the protagonist and acting where the immobile Chancellor acts only through the apparatus. The reference is the character played by Christoph Waltz in Inglourious Basterds, chiefly as an image of professional danger. German origins are proposed by Sasha but do not determine moral traits. [П6]

OPEN GAP: whether this is one character or two separate figures are needed, and his name, original service, and relationship with the mentor. The post-revolutionary conflict in Ivan's version and the conflict during the revolution in Sasha's version belong to different architectures of the whole story.
