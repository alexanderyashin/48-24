IVAN'S PROPOSAL. An agent arrives in the capital under cover of trading in rare mechanical components. His task is to deepen divisions and prepare an attack that can be blamed on Germany. Aristocratic manners, coldness, cynicism, and professional exploitation of other people's conflicts are proposed. [П3]

His immediate interest is to fulfil his service's assignment and alter relations between the continental powers. Ivan proposes an internal conflict between the mission and unexpected humanity: the decency of someone he is using, or an emerging personal attachment. Neither the specific object of attachment nor the operation's outcome is supplied. [П3]

OPEN GAP: name, superiors, Britain's position in the chosen geopolitics, real degree of independence, and connection to the Romanovs. He should not automatically represent all British people or know every conspiracy in the world. His operation depends on a configuration of Russia, Germany, and Britain that has not yet been agreed.
