JOINTLY DEVELOPED. This is a social niche for future characters, not an industrialist who has already been created. Private manufacturing districts and factory towns form the empire's resource base; their initial growth, subsequent deterioration, conspiracy, or revolt are discussed. An owner may be an ally of the authorities, a competitor of the bureaucracy, and an opponent of changes in ownership at different times. [П7]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Interests to test in development: control of the enterprise, access to raw materials and workers, predictable deliveries, protection of investment, and the ability to dispose of income. These interests may diverge from those of other industrialists and the government; they do not establish a ready-made party position. [Р1]

OPEN GAP: name, company, specific district, origins of ownership, political rights, and participation in repression. Relationships with engineers, workers, and smugglers should be determined by the economic model. It cannot yet be assumed that all owners support either the Chancellor or the revolution.
