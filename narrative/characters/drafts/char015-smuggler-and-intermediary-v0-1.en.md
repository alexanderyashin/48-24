SASHA'S FRAME. Smugglers should have a prominent place in the economy and adventures. Grey-market coal exports, illicit luxury imports, drugs, and possible trade across internal borders are distinguished. Sasha proposes treating the smuggling milieu as a counter-elite. No character or single organisation has been approved. [П7]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. For a particular intermediary, define the cargo, route, client, protection, and dependence on official authority. Personal profit, professional reputation, loyalty to a community, and political involvement can then clash concretely. Smuggling does not automatically equal liberation, nor does it create one shared worldview among all participants. [Р1]

OPEN GAP: name, network scale, connections to industrialists and Cossacks, access to foreign markets, and participation in supplying the revolution. Rules governing borders and trade must exist before a specific scheme for breaking those rules.
