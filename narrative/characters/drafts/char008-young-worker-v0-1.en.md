IVAN'S PROPOSAL. A young worker lives in poverty, sympathises with the left, and considers taking part in street action. His initial quality is kind-heartedness; the desire for change coexists with a readiness to help an individual person. He rescues a disguised noblewoman, taking her for a girl from his own social circle. [П1; П2]

Interests that follow directly from the outline: improving life and retaining the capacity for compassion amid confrontation. His trade, enterprise, organisation, and political programme are not yet specified, however. The word ‘leftist’ does not automatically make him a member of the future Union or an associate of the intelligence officer. [П1]

OPEN GAP: name, age, family, professional qualifications, consequences of meeting the noblewoman, and place in the larger cycle. A choice is needed between an independent protagonist in the personal strand and a member of a wider ensemble. His gentleness should not predetermine either political weakness or moral infallibility.
