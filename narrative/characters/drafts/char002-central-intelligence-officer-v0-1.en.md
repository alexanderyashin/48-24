SASHA'S FRAME. The continuing protagonist of the epic, the person through whom service to the empire, revolution, and the construction of the next order are connected. JOINTLY DEVELOPED: around twenty-five in 1941, service in foreign intelligence or its special unit, high professional aptitude, and initial pride in serving the Chancellor. His exceptional nature is discussed, but specific abilities and the route of his training are unestablished. [П1; П4–П6]

His interests shift from survival and duty towards independent action and creation. Sasha requires accumulated disillusionment through major events and a positive programme before revolutionary victory. The mentor's killing is proposed as a personal breaking point. The later part of the arc turns the protagonist into the guardian of his own order. [П4–П6]

DIVERGENCE: neither his name nor identification with Lieutenant Rzhevsky is fixed. Rzhevsky appears in the noblewoman's early strand; visual references to the screen version of Holmes and Yura Borisov establish no biography and imply no production agreements. OPEN GAP: social origins, exact rank, relationships with the physician and worker, positive programme, form of power, and personal ties.
