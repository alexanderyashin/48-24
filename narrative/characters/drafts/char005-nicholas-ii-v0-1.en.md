JOINTLY DEVELOPED. The historical monarch within a fictional alternative history. He loses effective power after Stolypin's survival; the degree of willingness, coercion, and understanding differs between versions. Ivan proposes isolation of the court, filtered information, and the tsar's gradual reduction to a ritual symbol. [П2–П4]

IVAN'S PROPOSAL. Nicholas experiences humiliation, depression, and a desire to restore his power; he is initially the person offered transformation into the Eternal Tsar. EARLIER VERSION: his brain or head survives in a throne installation and serves the conspirators. Alexei subsequently becomes the candidate for the mechanised monarch. [П3]

DIVERGENCE: Nicholas's death, continuation of his nominal rule, and his connection to the plan to save the heir. The date of death, Stolypin's responsibility, the public account, and religious consequences are undefined. His relationships with the empress, Rasputin, and aristocrats must be developed within the chosen version rather than automatically transferred from incompatible variants.
