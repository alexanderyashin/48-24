JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Cossack republics, independent communities, carriers, and groups capable of piracy on resource routes are proposed in the Wastelands. Individual Cossack stories are allowed as independent adventures, including comic episodes. No named ataman or fixed political programme exists. [П3–П5; П7]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. The future character's interests should be connected to a specific territory, source of income, armed force, and relations with neighbours. Freedom, imperial service, protecting a route, and robbery may belong to different groups or different periods. Origin does not replace motivation. [Р1]

OPEN GAP: the settlement's status, rights to land and arms, attitude towards factory districts, and functions in the revolution. This niche must not automatically be identified with the ruler of the Wastelands in the proposed second protagonist's version: that connection has not yet been made explicitly.
