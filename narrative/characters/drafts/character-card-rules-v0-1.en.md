## Rules for character cards

The cards record functions, interests, traits established in discussion, and open decisions. A card's existence does not require that character to appear in the first work. An unnamed social niche does not count as an already created character. The words ‘established’ and ‘proposed’ refer to the coauthors' correspondence; the historical accuracy of real prototypes is checked separately. Mutually exclusive variants are retained in a single card only as an explicitly marked divergence.
