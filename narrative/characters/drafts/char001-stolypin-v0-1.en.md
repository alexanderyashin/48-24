# CHAR001 Pyotr Stolypin

[Русский](char001-stolypin-v0-1.md) · [Wiki EN](../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md) · [SPOT 0.1 §13](../../../world/world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#char001)

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. The central figure of the first historical cycle: a statesman who survives an assassination attempt, the effective ruler, and the gradually mechanised guardian of his own order. His survival in 1911, rule by 1941, and incomplete mechanisation at the beginning of the main action are established. His official powers, titles, and relationship to any surviving monarchy depend on the choice of state structure. [П2–П6]

Proposed interests: preserving Russia and the results of his reforms, preventing chaos, and ensuring continuity of government. Ivan adds fear of death, inability to entrust his legacy to a successor, atonement, and the voluntary sacrifice of humanity. Sasha connects final mechanisation with a harder order, repression, and loss of the ability to tolerate uncertainty. These motives may coexist, but their hierarchy has not been chosen. [П4–П6]

Relationships: Nicholas is the former source of legitimacy; Rasputin is a proposed opponent or instrument; the mentor is a possible longstanding ally; the intelligence officer is first an agent and then an opponent; the security chief is a possible active representative of the system. OPEN GAP: the particular quality that secured his initial success, the limits of his responsibility for crimes, the preservation of memory and will after mechanisation, and the circumstances of his final defeat.
