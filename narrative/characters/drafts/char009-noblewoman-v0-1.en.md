IVAN'S PROPOSAL. A young woman from court or aristocratic circles is tired of her position and ventures into the city during unrest. Disguised, she loses her escort and becomes dependent on help from a worker who does not recognise her background. In the early version her fiancé is named as Lieutenant Rzhevsky, with a trickster function. [П1]

Her initial interest is to move beyond an imposed life and gain independent experience. Her degree of disillusionment with her own class, willingness to surrender privileges, and attitude towards politics are undefined. Her rescue cannot be assumed in advance to turn her automatically into a revolutionary or one half of an established romantic couple. [П1; П2]

OPEN GAP: name, age, family position, court connections, personal responsibility, and the meaning of a possible romance. Whether Rzhevsky and the central intelligence officer are indeed the same person requires clarification. Her former environment must contain active ties so that crossing between worlds creates consequences.
