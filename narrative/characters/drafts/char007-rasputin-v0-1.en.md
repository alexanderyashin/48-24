CONSENSUS. Rasputin is included in the backstory before 1941. DIVERGENCE: Sasha proposes an opponent of Stolypin connected to the archaic court system; Ivan proposes a figure who can be blackmailed into undermining the monarch's reputation. Access to the court, religious symbolism, and the political use of trust matter in both versions. [П6]

SASHA'S PROPOSAL. Stolypin exposes or outmanoeuvres Rasputin, turns the intrigue's consequences against the royal family, accuses him of working for England, and executes him. This sequence belongs to the alternative plot. A claim that he was a foreign agent in reality must not enter the historical account without independent confirmation. [П6]

OPEN GAP: Rasputin's personal objective, degree of belief, independence from the court, actual connection to foreign services, and role in a church schism. Editorially, he needs concrete resources and limitations: ‘chthonic’ expresses an authorial association but does not replace an operative motivation.
