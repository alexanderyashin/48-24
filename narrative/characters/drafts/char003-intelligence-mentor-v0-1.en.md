JOINTLY DEVELOPED. A proposed senior operative and longstanding ally of Stolypin, familiar with the secret circumstances of the regime's establishment. Sasha proposes the scale of a military intelligence chief or former head of a special department. His shared service with the Chancellor explains access to the past and his ability to influence the protagonist. His exact position depends on the decision about the number of intelligence and security services. [П5; П6]

SASHA'S PROPOSAL. He notices the inhumanity of the new course and becomes dangerous through knowledge of the past or disagreement. His removal prompts the protagonist's open break. A version in which he warns the protagonist at the cost of his own life is discussed alongside a literary reference, so no specific scene of self-sacrifice can be treated as established. Ivan explicitly retains reservations about the mentor strand. [П5; П6]

OPEN GAP: name, personal interest, degree of involvement in the destruction of the Romanovs, attitude towards revolution, and limit of loyalty. Kinship with the protagonist is not established. Execution for knowledge, punishment for objection, and elimination for operational reasons must be distinguished: these are different bases for the conflict.
