SASHA'S PROPOSAL. Scale: an intermediate underground arc. The protagonist joins disaffected people, but the participants fall out and the conspiracy is exposed. The outline shows that a common enemy is insufficient without compatible goals. The factions, subject of disagreement, source of exposure, and losses are unnamed. The failure should arise from the participants' actions and incompatible demands; the correspondence supplies no ready-made culprit, traitor, or saviour. [П6]
