SASHA'S FRAME. Scale: a historical cycle and many possible episodes. The empire enters a major war that exposes organisational weakness and contributes to the destruction of the old order. The initial duration of about twenty years and later shorter estimates have not been reconciled. The enemy, war aims, front lines, causes of defeat, and the intelligence officer's specific missions are undefined. The war must affect the economy and social coalitions. [П1; П2; П4–П6]
