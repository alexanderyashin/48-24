DIVERGENCE. Scale: an independent political arc before 1941. Rasputin either resists Stolypin as part of the court system or is used through blackmail to discredit the monarchy. In one proposal he is accused of an English conspiracy and executed. His actual connection to a foreign power, the empress's position, church circles' participation, and the sequence in which the audience learns the facts are undefined. The two versions should not be treated as a single plan without an additional decision. [П6]
