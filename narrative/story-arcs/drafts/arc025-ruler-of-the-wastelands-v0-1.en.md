IVAN'S PROPOSAL. Scale: the second main protagonist's long arc. A domestic security officer becomes an independent force in the Wastelands, supplies revolutionaries, and later clashes with the intelligence officer's new order. The sequence is expressed as a question, not an approved decision. Reasons for the transition, the nature of his power, relationships with local communities, and the timing of confrontation are undefined. This strand is incompatible with automatically assigning the same person the role of an unchanging repressive opponent in the first cycle. [П6]
