IVAN'S PROPOSAL from an earlier version of the world. Scale: a tragicomic journey arc. A physician takes his ill wife to the capital, concealing her in a coffin and sustaining her life. On the road, hope of treatment may turn into an intention to spread infection and die. The nature of the illness, the wife's agency, quarantine rules, and outcome are undefined. A connection to the unification of political sides was proposed within the early model and requires a new decision if the infection remains local. [П1]
