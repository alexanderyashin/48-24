IVAN'S PROPOSAL. Scale: an espionage arc. A British operative under commercial cover organises an event intended to worsen relations between Russia and Germany. The plan is complicated by personal attachment or a participant's unexpected decency. The event, perpetrators, aims of specific Russian intermediaries, and outcome are undefined. The entire strand depends on the chosen international configuration and does not prove that Britain directs every internal conflict in the empire. [П3]
