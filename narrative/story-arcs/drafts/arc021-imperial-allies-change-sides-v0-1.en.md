SASHA'S FRAME. Scale: a continuing strand involving several characters. Former imperial employees and supporters move into opposition when the state begins destroying the conditions of their lives and work. The protagonist connects this process, but does not create it through personal influence alone. Who changes sides, who refuses to support the revolution, and the precise limits of loyalty are undefined. This strand supplies continuity of skills and institutions while preserving the possibility of conflict between former allies. [П6]
