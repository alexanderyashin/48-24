DIVERGENCE. Scale: a dynastic backstory arc and possible continuation. Variants include isolation of the family, its death, the heir's abduction, and a mechanised monarch as a source of competing legitimacy. A writing machine's signature is proposed as the means of revealing the sovereign's identity. Victims, perpetrators, chronology, public account, and Alexei's presence in the world of the main action are unestablished. Machine-Nicholas belongs to an earlier version. [П3; П4]
