IVAN'S PROPOSAL. Scale: an adventure story. Smugglers carry secret cargo on a modified steam locomotive or small airship through dangerous territory, evading agents and local armed forces. Drawings, a scientist, and a technological sample are alternative contents. Route, client, transport, characters, and the cost of losing the cargo have not been chosen. The story should rest on an existing economic route rather than an arbitrary empty map. [П3; П7]
