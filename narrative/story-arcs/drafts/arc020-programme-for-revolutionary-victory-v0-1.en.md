SASHA'S FRAME. Scale: the concluding arc of the first cycle. The protagonist forms a small motivated group united by a conception of a new order, and achieves revolutionary victory with it. A positive programme distinguishes this stage from earlier attempts. The participants, terms of their agreement, organisation of mass support, limits on violence, and role of future production corporations are unestablished. The specific victory over the Chancellor and his apparatus has not yet been written. [П6]
