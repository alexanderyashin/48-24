JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Scale: an arc running through the first cycle. Stolypin undergoes gradual mechanisation, retaining some humanity in 1941 and becoming harsher as the crisis develops. Final immobility coincides with the most repressive stage of his rule. The operations, their causes, effects on his capacity to choose, engineers' involvement, and public attitudes towards the transformations must be defined. Physical replacement of body parts should not be the sole explanation for political policy. [П4–П6; Р1]
