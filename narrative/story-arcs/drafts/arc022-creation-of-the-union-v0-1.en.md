SASHA'S PROPOSAL. Scale: the specific organisation of the next historical cycle. After overthrowing the old authorities, the victors build a society based on workers' production associations and continue the external struggle. Guild-like features and a map of enclaves were proposed from the beginning. Transfer of ownership, rural participation, state bodies, the economy of common services, and the protagonist's position are undefined. The names Republic and Union still require agreement.
