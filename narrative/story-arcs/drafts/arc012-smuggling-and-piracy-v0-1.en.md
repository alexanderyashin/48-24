SASHA'S FRAME and JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Scale: a family of local stories. Grey-market coal deliveries, illicit luxury imports, and other prohibited cargo create interests for intermediaries, officials, and attackers along the routes. Ivan connects the adventures with industrial caravans and free Cossack communities. Internal customs borders, laws, cargo owners, and carriers' political role are undefined. Different commodity flows should not be reduced to one universal criminal organisation. [П7]
