## Purpose of the catalogue

The catalogue contains twenty-five proposed strands of different scales. A historical cycle, a serial arc, an individual story, and an allowed type of spin-off are explicitly distinguished. An entry does not mean an approved script or a production commitment. Statuses refer to the source material. It is especially important to keep the early infection outlines separate from the later local outbreak, and not to combine two incompatible versions of the second protagonist.
