SASHA'S FRAME. Scale: the final cycle and conclusion of the epic. The protagonist becomes the guardian of the order he created; his biography and the work end with his death. This is the boundary of the overall composition, separate from the specific geopolitical resolution. [П4; П6]

SASHA'S PROPOSAL. The society created by the protagonist achieves a major external victory, after which the union disintegrates. The scale of victory varies between the continent and the entire world. The internal mechanism of disintegration, attitudes of peoples and corporations, the protagonist's legacy, and the sequence of death and disintegration are undefined. A hint at a transhumanist future remains a proposed sign of the next era. [П1; П4; П6]
