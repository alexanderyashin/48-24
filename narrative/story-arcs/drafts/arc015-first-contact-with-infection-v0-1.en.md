EARLIER VERSION. Ivan's proposal. Scale: an opening horror scene. A landowner assaults a woman dependent on him, then abandons her and takes shelter when infected people appear. The scene ends with the threat of the shelter being destroyed. Its function is to show domination, refusal of help, and the arrival of a shared danger. The editorial account preserves these functions without eroticising violence. Neither the use of zombies nor the scene itself is established in the later canon. [П1]
