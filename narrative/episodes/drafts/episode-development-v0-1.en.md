# Work formats and instalment development

<a id="prod_scale"></a>

## The scale of the world and the size of an individual work

Status: SASHA'S FRAME and JOINTLY DEVELOPED. The world is intended for a large epic cycle spanning several eras. An individual work may cover a brief event and a limited cast. Publication order need not match the world's chronology. Backstory, main narrative, and side stories must retain shared rules and consequences.

The initially proposed starting form is text outlines and AI-generated video sketches of around thirty seconds. This reference point does not determine the length of all future works. The specific platform, release frequency, budget, team composition, and production method are unagreed.

Ivan requires consideration of broad accessibility, spectacle, and production feasibility. These requirements should be tested against a specific instalment: how many locations and characters must be shown, what actions the viewer must understand, and what information must appear in the frame. The entire body of canon is not to be transferred into dialogue or voice-over.

## Levels of material

| Material | Function | Minimum result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World bible | Maintain shared rules and consequences | An assessable proposition with status and links |
| Cycle bible | Describe a major change in the state of society | Initial state, transitions, outcome, and open dependencies |
| Character bible | Maintain consistency of the person | Interests, knowledge, constraints, relationships, and stages |
| Story card | Record the promise of an individual story | Participants, conflict, choice, outcome, and chronological position |
| Script | Determine the sequence of observable events | Scenes, actions, lines, and information revealed |
| Production card | Prepare visual execution | Location, appearance, lighting, props, sound, and duration |
| State register | Preserve the consequences of published material | Changes in the world, the fates of objects, and characters’ knowledge |

Status of the levels scheme: EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. It helps distinguish canon, story, and staging. The coauthors separately define authority to approve each material.

<a id="prod_first"></a>

## Assessing suitability as the first story

Status of the criteria: EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. The first narrative story is proposed to give the viewer a foothold in the world and a complete small-scale experience: an intelligible conflict, distinct participants, and an observable consequence. The choice between a story, teaser, and atmospheric sketch remains open. The promised tone must be specified separately: an intelligence adventure, industrial conflict, political horror, black comedy, or personal drama.

The first plot remains an open choice. Murder on an airship, an operation in an industrial district, carrying an unusual cargo, and the physician's story are different candidates with different requirements for the maturity of canon. None is designated the pilot instalment by this document.

| Criterion | What must be shown or defined |
| --- | --- |
| Clarity | The viewer understands a participant's immediate goal without a preliminary lecture. |
| Self-containment | The episode has its own change of state even if the larger conflict continues. |
| Connection to the world | Significant conditions arise from the economy, institutions, or technological rules. |
| Character | A participant makes a choice through which they can be understood. |
| Tone | Humour, cruelty, and dramatic consequences do not contradict each other by accident. |
| Continuability | After the instalment, it is clear which questions or relationships may develop further. |
| Staging | The number of scenes and visual complexity fit the chosen duration and resources. |

<a id="prod_audience"></a>

## Audience age and limits of depiction

Status: DIVERGENCE. The initial conception combines the term young adult, an interest in a young mass audience, and distinctly adult themes. A separate editorial decision on the target audience is required. A fast pace and young protagonist do not automatically determine an age category.

Agreement requires distinguishing an event's existence in the world, its description in text, and the degree of visual depiction. The regime's cruelty, sexual relationships, coercion, humiliation, and the consequences of violence should be assessed by their function in the specific story. Early scenes of violence remain in the catalogue as plot outlines; this creates no obligation to reproduce their original level of detail.

Until the overall approach is defined, record separately the characters' ages in each scene, level of explicitness, target of humour, and consequences of the action. This maintains consistency of the artistic decision and allows differently formed works within one universe without accidental changes to its audience.

## Instalment titles and recurring elements

Status: SASHA'S FRAME. Titles based on Russian obscene sayings are proposed for individual parts. This is a linguistic and tonal principle, not a ready-made list of titles. Its application to short videos, stories, cycles, and public announcements requires agreement.

Shustov should be a recurring sign of the commercial environment. The exact frequency, form of advertising, condition of products, and different classes' attitudes towards the brand are determined in scenes. EDITORIAL PROPOSAL: repetition retains material credibility—the inscription exists on a surface, a container has an owner and purpose, and an advertising promise corresponds to whoever pays for it.

<a id="prod_references"></a>

## References and the project’s independence

Status: SASHA'S FRAME regarding the use of references; methods for assessing independence are an EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Cole and Bunch's «Конец империи» (the title cited in the source) is named as the main reference for combining adventure, social mechanisms, and a large historical arc. Star Wars, ancient epic, and other works indicate scale or individual functions rather than a ready-made plot to transplant.

For each reference, record the specific aspect of influence: rhythm, point of view, ensemble construction, character of humour, investigative structure, or the material quality of the image. Sequences of events, individual biographies, proper names, and distinctive scenes require independent authorial development.

The independence of ‘Cyberstolypin’ is proposed to be assessed through conflicts arising from its own world. Manufacturing districts, rights to infrastructure, the monarchy's legacy, state services, the Church, and technologies must actually change characters' decisions. These elements must not remain a change of scenery around an unchanged borrowed plot.

<a id="prod_blanks"></a>

## State of the production plan

OPEN GAPS: the main medium of the first release; pilot duration; a fixed or changing cast for short instalments; narration by a storyteller; subtitle format; music and sound motifs; a set of reference images; permissible scale of crowd scenes; release plan; and feedback method. Dates, money, and production roles have not been assigned.

These questions can be resolved after selecting the first script experiment. Each decision should record its artistic reason and an assessable production constraint. Equipment and tools are chosen to achieve the result; they do not define the world's canon.
