# Principles of dramaturgy and voice

## Authorial requirements

SASHA'S FRAME. Characters, machinery, social forces, and speculative premises must have a function. The principal characters are connected to something larger than an individual adventure; their changes reflect changes in the forces they represent. The continuing protagonist has a particular freedom to move between positions. These requirements describe an author's intention and need to be applied to specific scenes. They are not universal laws prohibiting other forms of literary narrative. [П1; П2]

SASHA'S FRAME. Internal causal continuity matters: an innovation should change the world consistently, and social conflict should arise from living conditions and interests. EDITORIAL PROPOSAL: when assessing a scene, ask what each participant wants, what is available to them, why they choose this particular action, and what consequence remains afterwards. Function can then be assessed without reducing a character to a diagram. [П1–П7; Р1]

<a id="nar0702"></a>

## Details and a recognisable world

SASHA'S FRAME. Shustov cognac should be a constant, recognisable presence in the environment and a metaphor for advertising and capitalism. This is an explicit request for a visual and narrative motif; historical claims about advertising methods are checked separately. Its presence may connect distant places through signs, commerce, and characters' habits, but its specific appearances have not yet been approved. [П7]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. A detail can serve atmosphere, social distinction, recognition, rhythm, or the credibility of everyday life. Not every object must become an instrument of the resolution. An especially emphasised object does, however, create an expectation that must be handled. This distinction preserves the requirement for meaningful content while leaving the world room to exist beyond the immediate plot. [Р1]

## Black humour and tragic consequences

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Black humour, grotesque, and an adventurous pace form part of the initial promise. Sasha proposes naming individual instalments after classic Russian obscene sayings; neither a list nor naming rules has been created. Ivan argues for everyday and lighter episodes. The presence of a comic scene does not mean its consequences disappear from the shared history. [П1; П5]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Humour may arise from the gap between a solemn formula and its material outcome, professional confidence and limited knowledge, or a social role and an immediate reaction. Tragedy requires the cost of decisions to remain. If every instance of suffering is instantly turned into a joke, viewers struggle to know when the work asks to be taken seriously. The degree of coarse language should relate to the character and situation rather than be identical across the ensemble. [Р1]

<a id="nar0704"></a>

## Human, machine, and personal antagonist

SASHA'S PROPOSAL. An active bureaucratic opponent embodies the Chancellor's order and allows personal confrontations while the Chancellor is immobile. The protagonist should win through human flexibility and ingenuity. EDITORIAL PROPOSAL: identify the opponent's specific limitation that makes such an outcome convincing. The formula ‘a machine cannot be an agent’ is no substitute for chosen rules governing the preservation of consciousness and will. An unpredictable act by the protagonist must also have an intelligible personal cause. [П6; Р1]

<a id="nar0705"></a>

## References and independence

SASHA'S FRAME. Cole and Bunch's «Конец империи» (the title cited in the source) is named as the chief reference for combining action, humour, social mechanisms, and a protagonist's story within an imperial system. Other references concern individual functions: visual character, the type of antagonist, the detective-story form, or the scale of a universe. EDITORIAL PROPOSAL: use references as questions to ask about the quality of the project's own construction. Specific connections between Russian industrial geography, property, and changing eras should give the project an independent causal structure. [П2; П5; П6; Р1]

<a id="nar0706"></a>

## The second cycle and the ending

SASHA'S FRAME. The ending should reveal the limits of the protagonist's system and conclude his biography. EDITORIAL PROPOSAL: retain genuine differences between the regimes, the improvements achieved, and the irreversibility of the journey. Repeating a structural question does not require identical personalities, identical crimes, or an identical revolution. The cause of disintegration, the protagonist's personal outcome, and the surviving legacy must be developed separately. These three components of the ending remain open tasks, not the work's ready-made moral. [П4; П6; Р1]
