## The authors' loglines

SASHA'S FRAME. A careful paraphrase of the historical-cycle logline: a young officer serves Stolypin during war and imperial crisis, joins the revolutionaries, helps overthrow the old order and build a new state, and then becomes a conservative force in the following era himself. Later messages specify service in foreign intelligence and a gradual break with the system. This is a logline for an extended biography and a succession of regimes. [П2; П4–П6]

IVAN'S PROPOSAL. The logline for a human point of entry: a young worker seeking justice, a disillusioned noblewoman, and a physician trying to save his wife encounter a social revolution and must choose between their personal desires, relationships, and the dangers of a changing world. This formulation centres on three lives; their connection to the officer, Stolypin, and subsequent eras has not yet been established. It is a possible logline for an individual strand or an initial run of instalments. [П1; П2]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. A unifying logline for discussion: ‘In a coal-powered Russian Empire held back from revolution by the Iron Chancellor, a young intelligence officer and people from different social estates find themselves on opposing sides of a disintegrating order; their struggle creates a new state that will one day face the same questions about power, development, and the price of stability.’ This formulation combines the historical arc and the ensemble, but does not establish that all the named characters take part in one operation or belong to one revolutionary group. [Р1]


## Editorial variants from the initial import

The following formulations existed in the repository before this decomposition. They are retained as a distinct editorial layer for discussion; the exact SPOT content appears above. These variants are not new coauthor decisions.

### Logline options for authorial choice

Three different approaches from [SPOT 0.1, section 2](../../../world/world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-2) are retained here. The formulations below are AI-assisted editorial preparation and translation. They are **not verbatim quotations from the authors and not a new consensus**. Choosing a logline must not silently settle existing divergences about the protagonist, ensemble, first instalment, or ending.

#### A — a historical biography

**Source status: Sasha's frame.** Anchors: CAN017–CAN024, CAN032; DIV011, DIV016, DIV017. Suitable for discussing the entire epic; includes the direction of the full arc.

**English rendering of the Russian formulation:** In a coal-powered Russian Empire, a young officer moves from Stolypin's loyal servant to the creator of a new state—and eventually becomes the guardian of an order that the next era must overcome.

**English variant retained from the initial import:** In a coal-powered Russian Empire, a young officer rises from Stolypin’s loyal servant to the architect of a new state, only to become the guardian of an order that a later generation must confront.

**Before approval:** confirm a single continuing protagonist and the meaning of the second historical cycle. Do not treat this as settling the mechanism of revolution, the global scale of victory, or the future state's fate.

#### B — a human ensemble

**Source status: Ivan's proposal.** Anchors: CAN045, CHAR008–CHAR010; DIV011, GAP049, GAP052. May describe a separate cycle or an entry into the world; its connection to the intelligence officer is unestablished.

**English rendering of the Russian formulation:** A worker seeking justice, a disillusioned noblewoman, and a physician trying to save his wife find themselves inside an approaching revolution, where personal desires, loyalty, and the struggle to survive require incompatible decisions.

**English variant retained from the initial import:** A worker seeking justice, a disillusioned noblewoman, and a doctor trying to save his wife are caught in an approaching revolution, where desire, loyalty, and survival demand incompatible choices.

**Before approval:** determine whether their stories converge, what role the physician's wife's illness plays, and where in the shared chronology the ensemble acts. This version does not establish a global epidemic.

#### C — a shared universe

**Source status: SPOT editorial proposal.** Connects thematic directions without establishing a shared operation involving all characters. Suitable as an outline for describing the universe; less specific as the logline of an individual work.

**English rendering of the Russian formulation:** In an empire of coal and steam held back from revolution by the Iron Chancellor, an intelligence officer and people from different social estates find themselves on opposing sides of a disintegrating order—and create a future that must ask again about the price of stability.

**English variant retained from the initial import:** In an empire of coal and steam held together by the Iron Chancellor, an intelligence officer and people from across its social classes are drawn to opposing sides of a failing order, shaping a future that must confront the price of stability anew.

**Before approval:** decide whether this text is needed for the repository's front page, a pitch for the epic, or a synopsis for the first product. One text need not perform all three tasks equally well.

#### Recording a choice

The decision should name the selected wording, purpose, language, acceptable spoiler level, and coauthor confirmation. Rejected variants remain in the archive with reasons rather than disappearing from history. A logline for a particular video, book, or game receives its own card and does not automatically redefine the whole universe's logline.
