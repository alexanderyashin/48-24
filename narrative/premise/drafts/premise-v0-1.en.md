# Concept and premise of the 48/24 epic

## Subject of the work

SASHA'S FRAME. ‘Cyberstolypin’ is conceived as a large Russian epic about an alternative Russian Empire, revolution, and the eventual exhaustion of the revolutionary state. Imperial culture, industry, property relations, and social conflicts must form a functioning environment. Visual expression rests on the workings of that environment: coal blackens the cities, infrastructure determines prosperity, and modes of production create the interests of different groups. The names of historical figures and real place names provide the starting material for an independent fictional history. [П1; П2; П7]

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. The project's outward promise is spectacular adventure in a dark steam-powered world: intelligence work, the secret police, airships, factory towns, palaces, smuggling, and revolutionary clashes. Its underlying promise is to show how people make decisions within large social processes, and how the meaning of their victories changes over time. The shared universe connects stories through consequences, institutions, and recurring people. [П2–П7]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. A narrative episode can be assessed by clear stakes, a change in the participants' situation, and an intelligible outcome. This criterion applies to the chosen form of episode. Short atmospheric sketches can serve an independent purpose: introducing a setting, image, ritual, or mood without a plot resolution of their own. The overall scale of the epic allows both forms. [Р1]

## Thematic core

SASHA'S FRAME. The central theme is the role of the individual in history. The protagonist begins as an instrument of another person's will, becomes an independent actor, participates in transforming the state, and finally embodies the established order himself. Two major historical cycles—Stolypin's and the protagonist's—are intended to show the emergence, success, consolidation, and exhaustion of a social order. This is the author's model for the work, not a proven universal law of history. [П1; П4; П6]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. The thematic promise can be stated most precisely as follows: a solution that saved a society in one crisis may become an obstacle in the next. This formulation allows the genuine achievements of both regimes, the cost of preserving them, and the causes of further conflict to be shown. It requires concrete changes in society. The ruler's ageing, his cruelty, or the disappearance of an external enemy alone cannot convincingly express the entire proposed cycle. [Р1]

- SASHA'S FRAME. Order and chaos are poles between which imperfect people act. The principal characters should express social interests and worldviews while retaining differences in behaviour. [П1]

- JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Preserving the state, personal loyalty, professional duty, and the right to resist come into conflict within one biography. The turn towards revolution must have accumulated causes. [П5; П6]

- SASHA'S FRAME. The protagonist's victory does not end the historical process. The construction and subsequent exhaustion of the new order constitute a separate part of the epic; the protagonist's death is the proposed final boundary of his biography and of the work. The specific form of the state's disintegration belongs to a separate proposal. [П4; П6]

## Genre, tone, and audience

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. The working genre range includes alternative history, dark steampunk, action, political satire, tragedy, and occasional horror. Ivan proposes ‘steam-powered Gothic noir in the manner of Russian folklore’. Sasha names a darker version of Iron Sky as a visual reference. These formulas indicate a direction, but do not replace a separate decision about the balance of realism, stylisation, grotesque, and explicitly speculative premises in the world. [П3; П4]

DIVERGENCE. The discussion combines the label young adult, a wish to reach a mass teenage audience, and an intention to use adult sexual themes, severe violence, and harsh black humour. The primary audience, age rating, and permitted explicitness have not been agreed. Before scenes are developed, the audience for the main product should be defined, and whether spin-offs may use a different register decided separately. The central protagonist's proposed age of around twenty-five does not itself resolve this question. [П1; П2; П5]

SASHA'S FRAME. Epic scale does not require an unbroken solemn tone. Everyday scenes, comic side stories, stories about supporting characters, and local horror are allowed. Ivan stresses the need for breathing space between heavy events; Sasha explicitly supports this possibility. The proportion of humour, the narrator's position, and the limits of shifts from tragedy to farce within one instalment remain unagreed. [П4; П5]

## Short and extended premise

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Short premise: Stolypin survived the assassination attempt of 1911 and preserved the empire. By 1941, its life depends on coal, steam, and the order he created. As that order begins to destroy its own foundations, one of its best agents moves towards revolution and a form of power that eventually brings him to a similar historical limit.

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Extended premise: the thirty years following the Kyiv assassination attempt have brought Russia to a different political and technological modernity. Clean privileged districts stand beside industrial soot, distant manufacturing districts sustain the capital, and the ageing Chancellor's power becomes ever more closely integrated with the machinery of government. Intelligence officers, officials, owners, workers, physicians, nobles, and carriers act within this system for different reasons. War, accumulated contradictions, and repression alter their positions. The resulting alliance of the regime's opponents wins through a shared programme, then faces the separate task of building and preserving a new society. The premise records the direction of discussion; the alliance's exact membership and the sequence of crises remain open.

## The first product and the overall scale

SASHA'S PROPOSAL. Scene texts and short AI-generated video sketches, provisionally around thirty seconds each, are named as the first tangible step. Canon is envisaged as the internal basis for sustained work. This production format has not been established by a joint decision. Dates, budget, platform, season length, and production technology are not approved. An adventure episode may begin in any available era if its position and consequences relate unambiguously to the shared history. [П2; П4]

### Working premise of the 48/24 epic

**Purpose:** a common entry into the concept for authors, scriptwriters, and artists. This is an AI-assisted editorial condensation of the source document. It establishes no new plot and does not replace source proposition statuses. **48/24 is the project's working designation; its final title remains open.**

Source: [SPOT 0.1, sections 2–5, 12, 18–21](../../../world/world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-2).

#### Short premise

Stolypin survives the assassination attempt of 1911, and Russia follows a different historical trajectory. By 1941, its imperial order depends on coal, steam, industry, and increasingly mechanised power. People to whom this order has given a profession, position, and purpose gradually face a choice: preserve it or help destroy it.

In Sasha's proposed overarching arc, a young officer moves from service to the regime towards revolution and building a new state, then becomes the guardian of an order that the next era will begin to challenge. This second cycle is Sasha's authorial frame; its precise plot, dates, and state structure have not been approved by both authors.

#### Promise to the reader and viewer

Adventure, intelligence work, secret police, airships, factory districts, palaces, and dangerous supply routes are connected through the consequences of human decisions. The world should feel operational: fuel must be extracted and delivered, power depends on people and infrastructure, and a technological innovation changes relationships between social groups. Social conflict is revealed through characters' actions and lives.

This condenses the jointly developed direction in SPOT. The proportion of noir, satire, tragedy, horror, and grotesque remains a choice for each particular work.

#### Limits of this version

The final name, first product to release, pilot plot, and overall audience age have not been chosen. Final biographies, intelligence-service organisation, the dynasty's fate, the physical mechanism of mechanisation, and its spread are undefined. Global victory for the future Union, the state's specific ending, and the revolutionary calendar are unapproved.

The worker, noblewoman, physician, and intelligence officer cannot yet automatically be combined into one group. ‘Mechanical Alexei’ and ‘mechanical Stolypin’ require a separate compatibility decision. The early global epidemic, estate-based magic, and a Chancellor fully mechanised by 1941 are not to be used as quietly operative rules of the current version.

#### English working premise retained from the initial import

*Editorial translation for discussion; not an approved public synopsis.*

After Stolypin survives the assassination attempt of 1911, Russia follows a different path into the twentieth century. By 1941, its imperial order depends on coal, steam, industry, and an increasingly mechanised system of power. The people who have built their lives around that order must decide whether to preserve it or help bring it down.

One proposed overarching story follows a young officer from loyal service through revolution and state building, until he becomes the defender of a new order that a later generation will challenge. The setting can also support independent stories whose consequences remain consistent with the shared world.
