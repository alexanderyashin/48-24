# Fan Content Policy

**Project:** 48/24, a working title. **Version:** 0.1. **Effective date:** 19 November 2026.

We welcome your own non-commercial stories set in this fictional universe. You may publish qualifying literary fan fiction without asking us first or registering with the project. Please credit the setting and make clear that your story is unofficial.

This policy grants the limited permission below for rights the relevant project rights holders can grant. It accompanies [LICENSE](LICENSE). It is not a general licence for project assets or commercial adaptations.

## 1. What you may do without asking

You may write, translate your own writing, and freely share original literary fan fiction that is inspired by or set within the published 48/24 universe. This includes prose, poetry, text-only scripts and text-based roleplay stories. You may:

- Refer to the setting, its lore, places, events and established characters in your own words.
- Invent your own characters, plots and interpretations, including alternative-universe stories.
- Describe established characters and fictional objects in prose as part of your story.
- Publish the text on a fan-fiction website, personal website or social account, or offer a free text, PDF or ebook edition.
- Translate another fan author's story if that author has also authorized the translation.

Only the permission to use the protected underlying project elements is supplied here. Each fan author controls rights in their own original contribution. Normal hosting permissions reasonably necessary for a platform to display your permitted fan story are included; unrelated commercial adaptation rights are not.

You do not need to submit your story to this repository. A link to a story can be suggested to the maintainers for a community showcase, but inclusion is not guaranteed.

## 2. Conditions

### Keep it non-commercial

Do not sell the fan work or access to it, charge for commissions based on the protected setting, put it behind a paywall, offer it as a paid membership reward, or obtain sponsorship, advertising revenue or crowdfunding for it without separate permission. A free promotional sample for a paid adaptation is not covered.

Ordinary platform advertising is acceptable if you neither control those advertisements nor receive revenue from the fan work. A general personal donation link, unrelated to access, rewards, commissions or production of the fan work, is acceptable; funding tied to the fan work requires permission. If a project becomes commercial later, obtain permission before the commercial use starts.

### Credit the source and identify yourself

Place a readable notice with the work. For example:

> An unofficial, non-commercial fan work set in the 48/24 universe. Original project: https://github.com/alexanderyashin/48-24. Fan story by [your name or pseudonym]. Not endorsed by the project authors.

Use plain text for this attribution. You do not need an official logo. A translation of this notice is fine. Do not claim that your work is official, approved, canonical or written by the project authors.

### Respect the boundary between your story and official materials

Create your own text. Do not republish official chapters, scripts, dialogue collections, world-bible entries or other substantial official text, whether verbatim, translated or lightly rewritten. Short quotations independently permitted by law remain available on their own legal basis.

Do not package official assets with your story, use them as a cover, or imply that an asset download is permitted just because the story is free.

## 3. Uses that need separate written permission

This policy does not authorize the following uses of protected project material:

- Reproducing, redistributing, modifying, tracing, recolouring or making derivative redraws of official illustrations, concept art, maps, emblems, logos, layouts, textures, 3D models, audio, video, fonts or other supplied assets.
- Publishing visual adaptations of protected official character or object designs, including fan art based on those designs. Describing them within a permitted literary fan story is covered by section 1.
- Publishing games, tabletop systems or scenarios, comics, animation, films, audiobooks, dramatized recordings, merchandise, asset packs or other adaptations. These formats can be discussed as collaborations.
- Commercial publication or exploitation of protected project material or fan adaptations, including sales, paid commissions, sponsored productions and merchandise.
- Republishing, translating or distributing official project texts as substitutes for the originals.

The restrictions above apply only to rights actually protected and controlled by the relevant rights holder. An independently created image that merely uses an unprotected idea or general style, lawful criticism or parody, public-domain material, and other uses permitted by law do not become prohibited merely because they concern a similar subject.

## 4. Materials covered by the permission

The permission covers project-controlled published fictional setting elements and literary material to the extent necessary for the fan fiction allowed here. You do not need a special approval badge, a fan registration or per-story approval to rely on it for rights the granting rights holders control. It does not cover third-party works, another fan's original contributions, or rights belonging independently to a coauthor who has not authorized this permission. If a particular material is identified as rights-uncleared, disputed or restricted, this policy does not clear the missing rights; permission from the relevant rights holder is needed where legally required.

The project cannot grant rights belonging to another person. A historical figure, place or fact mentioned in the setting does not become project property. This policy makes no claim of ownership over general artistic styles or ideas, and it does not create copyright in material that is not legally protected.

## 5. Your rights and our canon

You retain the rights you hold in your own original text, original characters and other original contributions. You do not acquire ownership of underlying project materials. Posting a fan story, linking to it or allowing us to list the link does not give us a commercial adaptation licence to your story.

Fan fiction is unofficial and outside project canon unless the human authors make a separate recorded editorial decision and obtain any necessary contribution permission. An editorial decision alone does not transfer copyright. An AI assistant cannot approve your work or grant rights on behalf of a rights holder.

If you want your work developed as part of an official production, use the separate [contribution process](CONTRIBUTOR_TERMS.md). You may continue writing independent fan fiction without joining that process.

You may use writing tools to assist with a permitted fan story, subject to the same conditions. This does not provide a separate right to upload official artwork or restricted source assets to a tool, create a dataset of project materials, or train a model on protected project materials. Any such use needs an independent lawful basis or applicable permission.

You remain responsible for third-party material in the result and for accurately describing authorship when relevant. A tool's output does not establish that copyright exists or that all necessary rights have been cleared.

This section does not override permissions already granted to GitHub under its applicable terms or uses independently permitted by law.

## 7. Duration, corrections and future versions

The permission is worldwide, non-exclusive and free of charge for the qualifying non-commercial uses described here. For a fan work first shared while this version applies, you may continue using that version for that work, including corrections and translations, while complying with it. A later policy change does not by itself revoke permission for an already compliant work. New, independent fan works use the version in effect when first shared.

If you breach the conditions, permission for the infringing use is suspended until the breach is corrected. Where a breach can reasonably be corrected, we will normally identify it and give a reasonable opportunity to correct it before further action. This does not prevent action against deliberate infringement, unlawful uses or misrepresentation, and it cannot guarantee rights a third party never authorized.

For a licence beyond this policy, open a non-sensitive [licensing enquiry](https://github.com/alexanderyashin/48-24/issues). State the work, material, planned use, commercial model and requested scope. A human rights holder's express written permission is required where this policy does not cover the use; silence, a reaction, or an automated reply is not permission.
