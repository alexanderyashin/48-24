# The empire at a human scale

[Русский](human-scale-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §10](../../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-10) · Source status labels: SASHA'S FRAME; JOINTLY DEVELOPED; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_society_everyday"></a>

SASHA'S FRAME. A character’s social position should be expressed in interests and actions. In the original conception, principal figures are tied to classes, professions and worldviews, while the central protagonist can change his relationship to the historical process. These functions are an authorial method of constructing the ensemble. They do not replace a specific biography, professional knowledge, close relationships or possible conflicts between duty and personal need. [П1]

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. The contrast between wealthy cleanliness and workers’ soot is one of the most stable images. Society is nevertheless not fully described: between palace and penal labour there must be distinguishable ways of life if they appear in the narrative. Officials, soldiers, foremen, traders, physicians, workers’ families and rural residents do not automatically have the same poverty, loyalty or access to education. [П1, П4, П7]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. For every depicted environment, a short answer to everyday questions is useful: where a person sleeps, what provides heat, where food comes from, how work is reached, whom the person pays, fears and turns to for help. These answers create action and visual details. They show the cost of a political decision without a separate exposition of the entire social theory.
