# Religion in everyday life

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §10](../../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-10) · Source status labels: JOINTLY DEVELOPED; DIVERGENCE; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_society_religion"></a>

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. The church participates in the justification of power and the conflict surrounding monarchy. Its everyday role extends beyond the positions of senior clergy. Parishes, religious education, charity, family rites, burial and attitudes to industrial labour are not yet described. These forms should be developed through specific people and places without assuming identical beliefs among all inhabitants. [П4, П6]

DIVERGENCE. Human mechanisation raises questions that may take religious form: who counts as alive, who is responsible for an order, where treatment ends and replacement of personality begins, and what the ruler’s death means. Different characters’ answers need not coincide with the objective rules of the fantastic technology. A person may be mistaken, sincerely believe or exploit ambiguity for power; canon should distinguish these levels.

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. When creating a rite or ecclesiastical object, choose a real cultural foundation first and only then an authorial alteration. Unapproved symbols of a mechanical cult should not automatically be applied to the entire religious world. A final merger of church and machine-servicing has not been accepted in the correspondence and remains a possible but separate divergence.
