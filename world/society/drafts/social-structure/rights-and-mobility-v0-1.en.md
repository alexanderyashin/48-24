# Rights and social mobility

[Русский](rights-and-mobility-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §10](../../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-10) · Source status labels: OPEN GAP; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_society_rights"></a>

OPEN GAP. Estate rights, the position of former serfs and their descendants, land tenure, freedom of movement, labour contracts and possibilities for collective protection are not established. The opening scene with a woman dependent on a landowner does not determine the legal status of all rural Russia in 1941. To retain the real historical starting point, any change or restoration of earlier dependence after 1911 requires its own causal history. [П1, П7]

OPEN GAP. Women’s rights and access to property, education, professional work and independent political participation are undefined. The arc of a noblewoman leaving her familiar circle implies specific restrictions and possibilities, but these are not yet described. Distinguish family authority, etiquette, financial dependence and state prohibition: they are different sources of conflict and invite different forms of resistance.

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Social mobility should have a visible cost and be genuinely attainable. If a loyal, talented person can rise through service, this explains the regime’s appeal to the protagonist. If professional advancement requires a patron, it ties his career to mentorship. If the earlier route later closes, social change appears in the lives of new generations rather than only one officer’s personal disillusionment.
