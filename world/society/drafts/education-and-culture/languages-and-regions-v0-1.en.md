# Languages, peoples and regional differences

[Русский](languages-and-regions-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §10](../../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-10) · Source status labels: OPEN GAP; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_society_languages"></a>

OPEN GAP. Administrative and educational languages by region, service rules for different population groups, cultural autonomy and the position of local institutions are undefined. Real imperial geography makes these questions necessary but does not provide a single ready-made model for alternative 1941. Differences should be established from the selected region’s historical basis and subsequent decisions by the authorities.

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. A working regional profile should distinguish everyday language, institutional language, professional jargon and languages of external trade. Characters of the same origin may occupy different social positions and support different sides. Ethnic generalisations and harsh comparisons in the coauthors’ early discussion are not approved qualities of the world’s peoples. Costume, names, accents and rites need a specific cultural grounding; they cannot be replaced by one composite image of “Asia” or “the periphery”.
