# Education, language and cultural environment

[Русский](education-and-speech-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §10](../../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-10) · Source status labels: SASHA'S FRAME; OPEN GAP; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_society_education"></a>

OPEN GAP. Mass literacy, school provision, documentary language, professional terminology and the circulation of print across social groups are not established. Characters can read orders, newspapers, advertisements and technical records only in accordance with their biographies. The image of an uneducated mass must not automatically deprive specialists of the skills their work requires, while aristocratic status does not prove competence in every field.

SASHA'S FRAME. Russian speech, recognizable cultural forms, real names and obscene sayings should give the project its own voice. Individual story titles may draw on such sayings. The language of a state act, a factory conversation, polite society and the authorial narrator need not be identical. General permission for coarse speech does not erase differences in age, upbringing, profession and intention. [П1, П7]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Speech culture would benefit from a small environment-specific glossary: forms of address, offices, insults, technical terms, religious formulas, trading jargon and secret-service slang. It should explain meanings and contexts of use. Modern words from the coauthors’ working correspondence must not all transfer automatically into dialogue: some merely helped discuss functions of the future world.
