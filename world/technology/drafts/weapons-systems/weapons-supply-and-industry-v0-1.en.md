# Weapons, military supply and industry

[Русский](weapons-supply-and-industry-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §9](../../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-9) · Source status labels: SASHA'S FRAME; SASHA'S PROPOSAL; OPEN GAP; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_technology_military"></a>

SASHA'S FRAME. Military equipment should have a manufacturer, a clear method of use and a place in the economy. A steam tank is an important image, but its presence does not automatically determine army composition, tactics or the ability to replace every other weapon. Designs, model names, calibres, quantities and special capabilities are undefined. [П2, П7]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Initially, military material culture can be described by roles: personal weapons, unit support, heavy weapons, protected transport, communications, repairs and medical provision. Each role has a production and supply chain. Weight, wear, carrying, preparation for use and human reactions matter for depiction. Instructions for manufacturing weapons are not required in a working creative reference manual.

OPEN GAP. State arsenals, private manufacturers, standardisation, imports, ammunition sources and field-repair practices are not established. There is no decision on the differences between regular troops, district guards, Cossacks and underground groups. Avoid giving every participant equally serviceable and equally accessible equipment: differences should arise from supply and tasks rather than a ready-made game-class table.

SASHA'S PROPOSAL. The progressive side may use simple, accessible equipment while affluent conservatives use expensive, complex technology. Political position does not coincide with the degree of shine and equipment. This preserves social conflict without requiring a scheme in which “the new ideology owns all the new devices”. Specific means and uses remain matters for individual scenes. [П4]
