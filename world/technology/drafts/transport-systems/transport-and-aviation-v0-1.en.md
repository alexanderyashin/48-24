# Transport and aviation

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §9](../../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-9) · Source status labels: JOINTLY DEVELOPED; DIVERGENCE; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_technology_transport"></a>

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Steam trains, urban vehicles and airships make up the recognizable transport environment; horses remain important in rural areas. It is not yet established which journeys each serves, where the boundary lies between luxury and ordinary service, who owns terminals or who provides maintenance. Differences between the capital, an industrial district and the countryside must be preserved. [П2–П4]

DIVERGENCE. The absence of a mass-produced all-metal aeroplane is proposed as an explanation for airship dominance. This does not yet determine the fate of aircraft of other construction, engines or airfield infrastructure. Canon needs an answer about the purposes of aeroplanes and airships, not just airframe material. An airship may symbolize a long journey, serve as a military observation platform or provide prestigious transport; these functions entail different requirements and constraints.

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. A specification for a visible vehicle should answer questions about purpose, crew, boarding, supplies, servicing and available space. Departure time, difficulty in quickly changing route, dependence on a hub and social separation of passengers are especially useful dramatically. Neither speed nor payload is fixed by arbitrary numbers at this stage. Specific parameters are approved after choosing a historical prototype and the extent of its alteration.
