# Technical checks before introduction into a scene

[Русский](technical-scene-check-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §9](../../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-9) · Source status labels: OPEN GAP; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_technology_review"></a>

- OPEN GAP. Illuminating gas is currently described colloquially; the designation CO is not a complete specification of the product manufactured and supplied. A name, source and domestic infrastructure are needed.

- OPEN GAP. Mechanical eyes and instantaneous perception of the whole empire have no defined channels, delays or limits. A network of intermediaries cannot automatically be treated as the ruler’s direct vision.

- OPEN GAP. Punched tapes, computation and transmission of human thoughts are currently combined in one artistic image. Their functions should be separated before the plot depends on a precise property of a device.

- EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Historical brands are introduced after checking their existence in the relevant year, industrial profile and alternative fate. Apart from the separately specified Shustov, there is no common list of mandatory brands.

- EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Every new exceptional capability is checked for consequences for other industries and countries. If it changes the entire economy, it cannot remain an unnoticed personal possession of one protagonist.
