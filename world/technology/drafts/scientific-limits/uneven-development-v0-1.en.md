# Uneven development

[Русский](uneven-development-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §9](../../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-9) · Source status labels: JOINTLY DEVELOPED; DIVERGENCE.

<a id="world_technology_level"></a>

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. The world of 1941 has advanced steam technology, airships, mechanised industry and alternatives rarely seen in everyday life. Electricity and internal-combustion engines exist. The image of a blanket technological prohibition gradually gives way to explanations based on cost, infrastructure and distribution of access. Technological level should be specified by field, not a single scale of “thirty years behind”. [П2–П4]

DIVERGENCE. A short First World War and limited markets are proposed as reasons for a different developmental path, but do not yet explain every industry and country. It is not established why foreign manufacturers do not occupy available markets, how imports and patents operate, which technologies develop in the military sector or which remain outside mass consumption. Additional economic and political mechanisms require development.

HISTORICAL BASIS. Electrical enterprises and networks existed in Russia before 1911. The Porogi plant, commissioned in 1910, combined a hydroelectric station with electrometallurgy; its foundation also demonstrates the involvement of private capital and imported equipment. The alternative trajectory must therefore explain the subsequent spread of these capabilities rather than reinvent them after the divergence. [ИСТ02, ИСТ03]
