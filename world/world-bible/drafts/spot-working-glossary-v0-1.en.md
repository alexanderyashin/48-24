# SPOT working glossary

[Русский](spot-working-glossary-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** A normative draft for collaborative development. Authorial status applies to the exact source statement; migration and translation do not create new coauthor agreement. Translation awaits human review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §24](spot-0-1-import.md#section-24) · [Current canon policy](../../../studio/CANON_POLICY.md).

The glossary preserves the wording of the source snapshot. Kiberstolypin is the former working title; the project is now called 48/24. The source’s broad use of “canon” does not replace the current requirement for precisely scoped approval by both coauthors.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-24 block-0 -->

This glossary describes project terminology. Characters' language, names of institutions, official titles, and political self-designations require separate development and approval.

<!-- SPOT-0.1 section-24 block-1 -->

| Term | Working meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Alternate history | The development of the real historical world after a selected point of divergence. |
| Arc | Change in a character or system under the influence of events and choices. |
| World bible | A collection of rules, relationships, and materials supporting consistency across works. |
| Mining and metallurgical district | A production and social territory around interconnected enterprises; the parameters of its alternate version require development. |
| Iron Chancellor | An established label for Stolypin in the authors’ concept; his formal title and legal status need clarification. |
| Canon | World positions with a defined authorial status; in the narrow sense, the agreed operative foundation. |
| Kiberstolypin | The working title of the project; the public title has not yet been approved. |
| Counter-elite | A group with resources and connections capable of challenging the existing distribution of power; not a moral judgment. |
| Logline | A brief formulation of a protagonist, conflict, and significant outcome or risk. |
| Mechanization | An umbrella term for several technical alternatives for transforming the ruler that have not yet been reconciled. |
| Steam Sovereign | An early conception of the ruler; its identification with a specific character remains a divergence. |
| Wastelands | A working name for peripheral spaces and enclaves; their natural environment, borders, and the word’s in-world usage have not been approved. |
| Union of Socialist Corporations | Sasha’s proposed form of a subsequent production-based association; its constitution and exact name remain open. |
| Overarching protagonist | A character connecting a long historical arc; not necessarily the only bearer of a point of view. |
| Faction | A group with shared interests and a will to act; not necessarily a party. |
| Flashback | A depiction of the past outside chronological order; it may be subjective. |
| Era | A period with a particular social organization and its contradictions. |
| SPOT | A unified source of current positions, decisions, alternatives, and gaps. |
