# World rules and speculative premises

[Русский](world-rules-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §4](spot-0-1-import.md#section-4) · Source status labels: CONSENSUS; SASHA'S FRAME; JOINTLY DEVELOPED; SASHA'S PROPOSAL; DIVERGENCE; OPEN GAP; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL; EARLIER VERSION.

<a id="world_rules_baseline"></a>

## Baseline reality

SASHA'S FRAME. The world is an alternative Russian Empire with a divergence in 1911. Before that point it uses real geography, society, enterprises and historical circumstances; afterwards an author-created sequence of events unfolds. The main narrative entry point is 1941. Earlier history is revealed in fragments as needed, but remains the indispensable causal foundation of the world shown. The absence of a long historical prologue does not mean that objects, institutions and conflicts have no history. [П2, П4, П7]

CONSENSUS. Stolypin survives the Kyiv assassination attempt; his survival begins a different political trajectory. Choosing this divergence does not automatically make all subsequent proposals about conspiracies, British actions, the imperial family's fate or the structure of government true. Each element has its own status. Working materials must distinguish the historical biography before the divergence from the character's biography afterwards. [П2–П4]

SASHA'S FRAME. Real place names are retained as a basic principle. Inventing another planet, different human races or an entirely fictional Russia was rejected as the project's direction. Real names nevertheless do not guarantee identical borders, owners, enterprises or routes in the two versions of history. An entity that came into existence after 1911 in reality needs a separate explanation for its emergence in this world. [П7]

<a id="world_rules_speculation"></a>

## Extent of speculative departure

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Ordinary life is subject to recognizable material constraints: production, supply, labour, travel time, access to resources and skills. The fantastic is concentrated primarily in the unusual technological trajectory and the ruler's mechanisation. Coal-based energy, airships and cumbersome machines form the environment; preserving a personality inside a deeply mechanised body requires a separate speculative premise. It must not quietly grant the whole world unlimited capabilities. [П3–П6]

EARLIER VERSION. A system of inherited aristocratic magic opposed to left-wing technology was discussed in the first batch. Ivan subsequently explicitly says that magic has been abandoned, and the world develops through alternative history and technology. The early shields, healing by laying on hands and general rule that “the more technology there is, the less magic works” therefore do not enter the current set of character abilities. Restoring them would require a new joint decision. [П1, П2]

DIVERGENCE. The correspondence used “magic” in two senses: a character ability and an authorial premise that changes the historical trajectory. A reference manual must not conflate them. Surviving an assassination attempt is an event in an alternative biography; transferring consciousness into an autonomous machine is a fictional departure of a different scale. Both may belong to one world for the reader, but the authors need to understand which changes its physical rules.

<a id="world_rules_personhood"></a>

## What remains of the human being

DIVERGENCE. Three technically different images coexist in the correspondence: an organism with replacement body parts; a living brain sustained by an apparatus; and consciousness transferred into a computing machine. The last is not simply an enhancement of the first two. The first version of canon needs either a principal model or an explicit definition of these states as different stages, with costs and limits to the transitions. Until that decision, no scene should establish that transfer is reversible or easily repeatable. [П3–П6]

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. Stolypin's loss of mobility and dependence on the machinery of power have a dramatic function. At the beginning of 1941 he is still human, with some bodily modifications; complete mechanisation comes later. The precise implants, support systems and organs are unspecified. Nor is it established who may switch off the installation, repair it, alter communications channels or restrict access to the ruler. These technical questions also determine the actual political power of the people servicing him. [П5, П6]

SASHA'S PROPOSAL. The infertility of a mechanised Alexei was considered as a way to make him the last Romanov. This is a consequence of a proposed fictional operation, not an automatically accepted property of all mechanisation. Alexei's fate after the emergence of the Mechanical Chancellor remains open; his apparatus and the dynasty's fate do not transfer to Stolypin by default. Succession to power, biological offspring and reproduction of the technology itself are three different questions. [П3]

<a id="world_rules_limits"></a>

## Spread and limits of the technology

OPEN GAP. The cost of mechanisation, number of specialists, intervention time, patient suitability, failure rate, materials consumption, repair possibilities and operating life are undefined. The spread of the technology, methods of reproducing it and effects on lifespan also remain open. The authors need to establish which forms of mechanisation different groups and countries can access, who controls access and how the chosen scale changes society. Neither the uniqueness of the mechanised ruler nor the widespread availability of comparable solutions has yet been accepted as a property of the world.

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. The working specification for each fantastic device should record its purpose, input resources, operator, servicing, observable result, limit and failure mode. For a mechanised ruler it also needs the carrier of memory, means of perception, command channels and degree of dependence on intermediaries. This does not require formulas explaining nonexistent science: consistent rules that work equally in drama, comedy and adventure are sufficient. [Р1]

SASHA'S PROPOSAL. A local infection story in a Siberian village can coexist with the wider world as a separate horror tale. A mutated fungus and infected people dying from the cold were discussed; this is a plot sketch, not approved biology. Origin, transmission, symptoms and limits of spread are unspecified. A global catastrophe that eliminates imperial institutions belongs to the earlier competing conception of the world's origin. [П2, П4]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Causal discipline in the project means that exceptions have consequences and remain recognizable. It does not require explaining every mechanism to the audience before action begins. Mystery is possible if the authors know its limits; characters may be ignorant provided that ignorance does not replace an established rule with an arbitrary new ability.
