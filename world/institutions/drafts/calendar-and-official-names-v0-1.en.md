# The calendar and official names

[Русский](calendar-and-official-names-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §6](../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-6) · Source status labels: OPEN GAP.

<a id="world_state_calendar"></a>

OPEN GAP. The civil calendar and whether a calendar reform occurs after the divergence are undecided. A common editorial dating convention is needed, along with rules for letters, newspapers, orders and religious holidays. Until then, exact days and months remain subject to verification; the agreed years remain in place.

OPEN GAP. A register of the names of the state, offices, institutions, ranks, districts and settlements is needed. Official titles, nicknames and the authors’ working analogies must be distinguished: “kaiser”, “Iron Chancellor”, “our CIA man”, “king of the Wastelands”. Signatures, seals, forms of address and expressions of legitimacy depend on this.
