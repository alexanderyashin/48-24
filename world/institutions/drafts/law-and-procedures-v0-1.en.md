<a id="world_state_law"></a>

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Working scenes initially need just five procedures: who issues a binding order; who may stop a person; who authorizes search and seizure; who adjudicates property disputes; and where complaints go. The answers should change with the era while remaining recognizable. Bureaucracy then exists through action rather than merely an official’s costume and the word “repression”. [Р1]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. The degeneration of the apparatus can be shown as a diminishing capacity to correct errors: an emergency decision becomes routine; a temporary inspection becomes permanent surveillance; an economic failure becomes grounds to suspect treason. This is one possible sequence consistent with the transformation under discussion. Specific laws, executions and campaigns do not enter canon without separate approval. This approach preserves the personal responsibility of people within the apparatus and does not explain every action through an abstract machine’s will.
