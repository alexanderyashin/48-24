# Proposed seizure of powers

[Русский](seizure-of-powers-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §6](../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-6) · Source status labels: IVAN'S PROPOSAL; DIVERGENCE.

<a id="world_state_takeover"></a>

IVAN'S PROPOSAL. After the assassination attempt, an emergency chain of administration is created: local defence committees and governors personally loyal to Stolypin. An act for the protection of the state order is passed through the Duma, establishing a chancellorship with prolonged emergency powers. The imperial family is gradually isolated on security grounds; its orders and contacts pass through the chancellery. Russia's withdrawal from the war, economic successes and propaganda turn de facto control into an accepted norm. [П4]

IVAN'S PROPOSAL. In the expanded version, passing the act involves bargaining, blackmail and the removal of opponents, while isolating the monarch is accompanied by control of information. Later, the possible death of the dynasty is presented to society as a tragedy after which the chancellor remains the state's guardian. This is an authorial outline of a transfer of power. It does not describe established events in real history or automatically demonstrate the legal sufficiency of every step within the alternative version. [П4]

DIVERGENCE. Sasha proposes using Rasputin as a powerful opponent during the rise: exposing his manoeuvres, connecting him to a foreign conspiracy, removing him and assuming a new role as saviour. Ivan proposes first controlling Rasputin through blackmail and using him to discredit the court. In the first version the chancellor defeats a competing centre of influence; in the second he deliberately destroys the reputation of the monarchy he protects. A sequential combination is possible only after a separate authorial decision. [П6]

| Centre of power | What is already present | What must be defined |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The chancellor and his chancellery | De facto governance and a personal hierarchy | Signing orders, deputies and limits of delegation |
| Monarchy and court | Symbolic and religious legitimacy | Family membership, survivors and legal fate of the crown |
| The Duma | Instrument of legitimation in Ivan's proposal | Powers after the emergency act and composition of representation |
| Governors and local committees | Proposed parallel hierarchy | Survival into 1941 and relations with ordinary administration |
| Industrial owners | Private districts and economic influence | Taxation, courts, police, representation and limits of autonomy |
| The church | Legitimation and internal dissent | Autonomy, schism and positions on the mechanised ruler |
