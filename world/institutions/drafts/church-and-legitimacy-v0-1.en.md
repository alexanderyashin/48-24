[Source: SPOT 0.1, §6](../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-6) · Source status labels: JOINTLY DEVELOPED; DIVERGENCE; OPEN GAP.

<a id="world_state_church"></a>

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. The religious component of monarchy is recognized as significant to the backstory. The sovereign's anointed status makes removing the tsar a question beyond personal strength and parliamentary procedure. Ivan introduces clergy who support the chancellor and adherents of the traditional crown; the supplied fragment leaves the further structure of this conflict unfinished. The entire church cannot be presumed either a unanimous state apparatus or uniformly oppositional. [П4, П6]

DIVERGENCE. Support for the chancellor may arise from a promise of order, defence of the monarchy from fidelity to a religious understanding of power, and doubts about mechanisation from questions of the ruler’s personhood and accountability. These motives are offered as distinct positions for development, not as ready-made parties. Their prevalence, leaders and institutional expression are undefined. A separate question is whether religious authority can recognize an apparatus communicating through intermediaries as the continuation of the former person.

OPEN GAP. Relations between the state and other confessions, local religious institutions and nonreligious associations are not described. Imperial geography cannot be reduced to a single church milieu. The relevant rights and restrictions should be developed for a particular region, set of actors and period, grounded in the chosen historical baseline.
