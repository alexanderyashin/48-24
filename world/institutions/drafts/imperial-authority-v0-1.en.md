# The duality of imperial authority

[Русский](imperial-authority-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §6](../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-6) · Source status labels: SASHA'S FRAME; JOINTLY DEVELOPED; OPEN GAP.

<a id="world_state_structure"></a>

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. By 1941, actual governance of the empire centres on Stolypin and the order he has created. His power rests on successfully overcoming an earlier crisis, a long reign, the apparatus of coercion and the belief that he is irreplaceable. The precise constitutional designation of the regime is nevertheless undefined. The correspondence includes parliamentary and constitutional monarchy, a chancellorial dictatorship, guardianship after the dynasty dies, and a combination of a formal sovereign with a power behind the throne. These arrangements cannot yet be declared a single finished legal system. [П2–П4]

SASHA'S FRAME. Stolypin must undergo a rise, stable rule and the collapse of the order he created. The apparatus of 1941 therefore cannot be described solely as a machine for arbitrary violence. It must retain the functions that made people serve the state, take pride in it and consider it an improvement on the past. Its subsequent degeneration becomes intelligible through changes in the priorities and practices of existing institutions. [П5, П6]

OPEN GAP. The formal head of state, status of the crown, appointment of the chancellor, limits and termination of emergency powers, and succession to supreme authority are not established. Nor is it defined to whom soldiers and officials directly swear allegiance. These questions matter in scenes: an order may be formally lawful but substantively destructive, while resistance to it may simultaneously be fidelity to an original duty and a crime against the state.
