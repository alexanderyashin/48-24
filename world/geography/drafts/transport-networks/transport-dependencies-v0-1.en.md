# Transport as a chain of dependencies

[Русский](transport-dependencies-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §8](../../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-8) · Source status labels: IVAN'S PROPOSAL; OPEN GAP; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_geography_supply"></a>

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Each important route is described from sender to consumer: the goods, cargo owner, transport used, transshipment points, guards and necessary permits. Seasonal restrictions, repairs, payments and available alternatives are then recorded. Exact distances, capacity and costs are added after research rather than filled with attractive invented numbers.

OPEN GAP. Actual supply routes for fuel, metal and food to the capital, the army’s dependence on particular factories and the role of ports in imports are undefined. It is unclear which lines existed before the divergence, which the chancellor built and which remained projects. This is a separate research layer, not a problem solved by the phrase “brought from the Urals”.

IVAN'S PROPOSAL. Iron convoys offer an image of slow, valuable and vulnerable cargo movement. A map is mentioned in the correspondence, but its image is absent from the supplied materials; a specific route cannot be reconstructed from that mention. For the main era, the authors must establish the convoys’ relationship to railways: whether they survive on particular legs, serve special cargoes or represent an alternative economic policy. An eighteenth- or nineteenth-century historical route cannot be carried unchanged into 1941 without verification. [П7]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. A supply map should begin with one specific consumer and one critical material. For example, investigate a chosen production site’s dependence on fuel, then add the supplier and route. This produces a verifiable network fragment that can be expanded. Such a map simultaneously supports an economic scene, a carriers’ adventure and a political conflict while preserving consistent distances and consequences.
