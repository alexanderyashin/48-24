# The capital as an interconnected city

[Русский](capital-network-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §8](../../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-8) · Source status labels: IVAN'S PROPOSAL; OPEN GAP; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_geography_capital"></a>

IVAN'S PROPOSAL. Petersburg’s island layout gives bridges, crossings and access between districts political and narrative importance. Social contrast is expressed through affluent residential districts and industrial quarters. Their precise placement on actual islands and streets has not been mapped. The mechanised Chancellor’s residence, secret-service headquarters, industrial plants, supply stations and principal public spaces are undefined. [П4]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. A city map should connect at least five kinds of movement: people to workplaces, food to inhabitants, fuel to production, orders to their executors and waste out of residential environments. A blocked bridge then changes life in particular districts, while a captured station affects a specific network. A visually striking obstacle becomes an event with a limited, intelligible area of effect.

OPEN GAP. Population, supply volumes, the number of factories and route capacities have not been calculated. At this stage it cannot be asserted that a single line supplies the entire city or that losing one warehouse makes it cease to function. An initial scene needs only an established local dependency, without presenting it as the arrangement of the whole capital.
