# Physical geography and alternative history

[Русский](physical-map-and-alternative-history-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §8](../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-8) · Source status labels: SASHA'S FRAME; DIVERGENCE.

<a id="world_geography_map"></a>

SASHA'S FRAME. The project uses the real geography of Russia and the connected world. Mountains, rivers, distances, climatic differences and available natural resources form a stable foundation. Political borders, administrative names, property, settlements and infrastructure after 1911 may change in response to author-created events. A present-day map is not a ready-made map of alternative 1941. [П7]

DIVERGENCE. The capital is called both Saint Petersburg and Petrograd. The name depends on whether the corresponding renaming occurs in the new history and what it means to the regime. Until agreement, “the capital” is an acceptable working label, with Saint Petersburg providing the real geographical reference. Names that arose from the revolution and subsequent campaigns in reality require separate decisions. [П3, П4, П7]

HISTORICAL BASIS. Uralvagonzavod began producing railway wagons in 1936; its transformation into a tank factory is connected with the events of 1941 and evacuated production facilities. Retaining the location and name is therefore possible as an authorial choice, but the factory cannot automatically be treated as unchanged inheritance from 1911. Its emergence, capital, production profile and technological connections need an alternative history. [ИСТ06]
