# Agriculture and food

[Русский](agriculture-and-food-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §7](../../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-7) · Source status labels: SASHA'S FRAME; OPEN GAP; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_economy_food"></a>

SASHA'S FRAME. Stolypin’s agrarian policy and preference for private farming form part of the explanation for a different development trajectory. Farm size and composition, land distribution and the results of reform by 1941 are nevertheless not established. Rejecting the Soviet historical path does not automatically imply either preservation of every relationship of 1911 or a uniform model of small landowners’ estates. The positions of proprietors, tenants, rural communes and wage labourers need separate definition. [П2, П4]

OPEN GAP. Food-producing regions, yields, processing, storage, wholesale trade and the connection between agriculture and industrial settlements are not described. It is unknown who supplies the capital, army and remote districts, who pays for transport or how dependent local residents are on imported food. A mining and metallurgical enclave cannot be assumed self-sufficient simply because forest and ore surround it.

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. For an initial economic model, choose one kind of industrial settlement and establish the sources of its basic food, fodder and seasonal reserves. Then identify the participants controlling purchasing, storage and sale. This can portray a supply crisis through prices, quality and availability without immediately introducing empire-wide famine or a uniform rationing system.

OPEN GAP. Changes in rural labour caused by machinery, military mobilisation and migration to factories are not established. The political programme future revolutionaries offer the countryside, and its compatibility with the new Union’s production-based principle, are not described. If corporate membership becomes the basis of civic organisation, the position of an independent farmer and a rural family requires a separate answer.
