# Trade, smuggling and counter-elites

[Русский](trade-smuggling-and-counter-elites-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §7](../../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-7) · Source status labels: SASHA'S PROPOSAL; IVAN'S PROPOSAL; DIVERGENCE; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_economy_trade"></a>

SASHA'S PROPOSAL. The world needs numerous smugglers, grey-market coal exports, illegal luxury imports and special trading zones. Drug trafficking is mentioned separately and does not stand in for the entire shadow economy. Internal customs boundaries are discussed as a possible additional source of smuggling but are not established. [П7]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. For every grey-market chain, define the prohibition or restriction that makes it profitable: a duty, monopoly, scarcity of permits, quota, export ban or difference in domestic prices. It then needs a legal seller, intermediary, buyer and protection provider. Such a chain can explain corruption and the emergence of an independent counter-elite without a universal underground syndicate doing everything at once.

DIVERGENCE. “Offshore zone” is currently a modern working label for a desired economic function. Its actual form might be a free port, special concession, territory with a different customs regime or a legal haven for capital. None of these arrangements has been adopted. The authors must establish why the authorities permit the exception, who profits and which conflicts its continuation creates.

IVAN'S PROPOSAL. Piracy and free Cossack communities are linked to valuable cargo moving through weakly controlled territories. Historical river convoys carrying iron provide a visual reference. For the 1941 version, it remains to establish which goods travel by water, rail or air, why transport is vulnerable and how raids, smuggling, private guards and local authority interact. [П7]
