# Foundations of the economic model

[Русский](economic-foundation-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §7](../../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-7) · Source status labels: SASHA'S FRAME; JOINTLY DEVELOPED; OPEN GAP.

<a id="world_economy_basis"></a>

SASHA'S FRAME. The economy grows from the real enterprises and economic geography of the original empire, followed by alternative development. Historical companies may expand, change their product range, enter associations or disappear because of new circumstances. Their presence is more than a decorative name on a machine: an enterprise has an owner, workforce, suppliers, customers, knowledge and dependence on transport. [П7]

JOINTLY DEVELOPED. The main direction is private mining and metallurgical districts and industrial enclaves, particularly important in the Urals. They provide the empire’s resource base and space for adventure, local authorities, smuggling and future opposition. The term “patrimonial estate” conveys the proposed degree of economic enclosure and personal dependence, but does not by itself define the legal status of all districts. [П7]

OPEN GAP. The balance between private, state, communal and other forms of property is not established. Banks, credit, currency circulation, foreign debt, insurance and state procurement are undefined. It is unknown which enterprises share owners, which compete, which are protected by concessions and how their growth is financed. Without this information, neither the early regime’s prosperity nor the later crisis can be conclusively derived.
