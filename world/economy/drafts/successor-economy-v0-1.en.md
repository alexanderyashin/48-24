# The economy of the next era

[Русский](successor-economy-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §7](../../world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-7) · Source status labels: SASHA'S PROPOSAL; OPEN GAP; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_economy_successor"></a>

SASHA'S PROPOSAL. The future state is described as the Union of Socialist Corporations: productive associations owned by their workers, with their own territories, forming a patchwork of enclaves. The geography of membership follows production rather than solely national or territorial divisions. Later correspondence more often mentions a new republic, but its relationship to the original corporate arrangement is not clarified. [П1, П4]

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Private industrial districts may become the material foundation of these associations: factories, skills, settlements and connections persist, while property, representation and distribution rules change. This is a possible bridge between eras that preserves the world’s continuity. It does not mean simply renaming the former patrimonial estates: the authors must decide who counts as a worker and member, and how families, pensioners, new migrants and people outside production are represented. [Р1]

OPEN GAP. The Union’s common authority, taxes, intercorporate payments, army, right of withdrawal, allocation of shortages and dispute resolution are unspecified. It is not established how workers’ associations become political forces or what prevents them from entrenching new privileges. These questions are needed for the second historical arc, but their answers should not be prematurely imposed on the characters of the initial 1941 setting.
