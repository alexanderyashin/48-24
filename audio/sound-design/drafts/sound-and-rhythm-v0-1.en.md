# Sound and rhythm

[Русский](sound-and-rhythm-v0-1.md) · [Wiki](../../../studio/navigation/en/README.md)

**Draft.** Normative specification for development. Status labels apply to individual source propositions; source consensus is not new canon approval. Translation awaits coauthor review.

[Source: SPOT 0.1, §16](../../../world/world-bible/drafts/spot-0-1-import.md#section-16) · Source status labels: OPEN GAP; EDITORIAL PROPOSAL.

<a id="world_visual_sound"></a>

OPEN GAP. A sound canon has not been formulated. The initial images include steam, mechanisms, telegraphy and an artificial voice, but there are no decisions about music, speech patterns, density of sound or regional differences. This is an independent layer of the future short videos.

EDITORIAL PROPOSAL. Recognizable environmental operation may provide the basis: production rhythms, steam releases, transport movement, pauses in communications, and the difference between a quiet maintained interior and a place of continuous labour. Sound should originate from an operating object and change with its state. Musical quotations, folk motifs and church sounds are introduced with a specific function and cultural grounding; generic ceremonial music does not replace a place’s character.
